## The Volume of the Music

I doubt this experience would have come from a large dose of LSD alone.

You could even say it was an experience reachable only by way of a Kaio-ken with the danger dial wide open — DMT the day before, then twenty-four times the normal dose of LSD the next day.

(Kids, do not try this at home. Ever.)

Right then, I was listening hard to what T was saying.

But we were at a party, and fierce Goa trance was pouring out at ear-splitting volume.

It would be hard to find a worse place to sit and take in what someone is telling you.

"The music's too loud. Couldn't it get a little quieter?" The instant that crossed my mind, the volume slid down and down, and T's voice began to come through clearly.

"Wait, did the music stop?" When I tried to listen for the music, the volume climbed right back up and it was coming through perfectly.

"Ah, sure enough, it's playing." And I went back to concentrating on T.

From the instant I locked onto her voice, the volume started sinking again, exactly the same way.

W-w-what — was the DJ working the volume in sync with my consciousness?

No.

It wasn't that. In sync with my consciousness, the focus of my five senses had been raised to an extreme degree.

Everything but the volume of what I wanted to hear shrank away, and only what I wanted to hear came through clearly.

My brain had gone and rewritten itself to a very convenient spec.

## The Speed of the Music

I was obviously past the limit, too far gone, and yet, strangely, there was no fear.

Part of it was being surrounded by friends I trusted completely; you could also say I was too far gone to have any wiring left that could feel fear.

This extreme other-dimensional experience was interesting, so I tried focusing on the music.

This time, from the instant I thought "The music's fast," the speed of it began to drop.

And when I thought "Too slow," it picked back up. That was how it went.

In sync with the music, even the rate at which time moved was shifting.

Time was not something that existed absolutely, out there on the outside; it existed relatively, in its relation to consciousness.

When the sense of consciousness changes, the outside world changes along with it.

What was unfolding there was a world of another dimension, nothing at all like the world I had known until then.

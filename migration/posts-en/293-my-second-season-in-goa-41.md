## Y's Place

The party was over, but the LSD was still going at full blast, and I was still in the baby state.

It was probably somewhere around midnight, but every one of us was outside the concept of time.

I was in no shape to ride a motorcycle, and my girlfriend I-chan wasn't calm enough to ride with me on the back.

So I got on the back of Y's motorcycle, and all of us went to his place first.

Either way, nobody was in any condition to fall asleep right then.

We got to the house, passed a chillum around, and settled down for the moment.

Everyone at that party had gone past their limits, and each of us was shaken by it.

We'd kept talking through the party, but holding a conversation at that volume, that wrecked, is not easy.

Inside a quiet house, the talk can go deeper.

## Framework

Once you've had a deep experience of another dimension, the fundamental framework of how you see the material world changes.

That was happening to all of us, and each person's experience echoed off the others and amplified them.

We shared what each of us had been through, and the things I'd been through — the music changing however I wanted it to, the questions and answers with T, day and night switching back and forth — were trying to force the framework of our values into a new arrangement.

By that point I had regressed all the way into a baby, lying there and doing nothing but staring up into the air with pure, shining eyes.

The conversation came into my ears and I could understand it, but nothing in me responded to it.

That was more or less how we made it to morning.

## Going Home

I-chan had come down enough to take me home on the back of the motorcycle, so home we went.

I hadn't changed at all — still a baby.

There was no judgment in me about whether to go home, and getting on a motorcycle or not wasn't a question at the level I was on.

All I did was look out with those pure eyes.

I sat on the back as I was told, held on to I-chan tight like a child, and rode home.

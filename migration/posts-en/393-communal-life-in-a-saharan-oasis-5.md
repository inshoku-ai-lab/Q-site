## O's Tales of Daring

The tales of daring that O, the Australian, told about her travels often left me amazed.

Her looks, though, were the complete opposite of those tough tales: she was a young, pretty white woman.

She had none of Janis's loud, over-the-top toughness, and there was even something homey about her.

And yet she said she had hitchhiked all the way from London to Morocco without a penny.

Strictly speaking, she wasn't penniless; she seemed to have about 20,000 yen on her, roughly 160 dollars, but she was apparently keeping that for a last-resort emergency.

For all practical purposes, she was traveling with no money.

## Hitchhiking

A girl traveling on her own, getting all the way to Morocco on hitchhiking alone: that was a pretty stirring story.

She was living proof that anyone at all can travel the world freely.

I-chan, though, shot it down on the spot: "No way, no way. Absolutely no way." I can understand how she felt.

When people hear about hitchhiking, the first thing everyone asks is: Isn't it dangerous? Aren't you scared?

Her answer was that with experience, knowledge, and trust, it's no problem.

## Technique

For a woman to hitchhike safely, she said, there's just one rule to follow.

Avoid cars with a lone man at the wheel.

Her hitchhiking style was very simple.

She would watch a car come closer, get a sense of the driver's vibe, and only then put her thumb out.

If the driver gave off a good vibe and it was a family, a couple, or a group of friends, she'd put her thumb out without a second thought.

If the vibe was bad, though, or it was a man driving alone, she always let that car go by.

Stick to that one simple rule, she said, and there's nothing to worry about.

After that, all you do is trust the universe and pray the journey goes well.

And she really had been hitchhiking her way around with no money, safely, for years, so it was hard to argue with.

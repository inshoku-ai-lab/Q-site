## Next to Y

Y had apparently been in Tokyo only a few days before, and he still had the city about him: clean, polished, a little tense.

We, on the other hand, had settled right into the desert town and were completely at ease, so the contrast between us was kind of funny.

To us, his city polish felt a bit nostalgic, but part of it also got on our nerves a little.

It was a relic of the past, the very thing we had hoped to shed by going on the road and getting to know the desert.

For Y, though, how at home we were in the desert was something he'd never seen before: real travelers in the flesh, the embodiment of exactly what he had come to write about.

Then along came Gandalf, dressed like an old wizard, and when Y saw how friendly he was with us, his curiosity about us shot into high gear.

## The Old Wizard

Gandalf had come to invite us to join the crew putting the festival together.

Normally, people who work at the festival sign on as staff in exchange for the steep price of a ticket, but we already had tickets, so there was no need for that.

There'd be no pay, he said, but we could come and help out whenever we felt like it.

All we were doing was loafing around a small desert town, so it sounded like it might be fun, and we decided to give it a try, just a little, to see how it went.

The one who jumped at the idea was Y.

For a writer traveling in search of interesting experiences to write about, it was a chance that wouldn't come twice.

## Volunteering

The job we took on was decorating the chill-out area Gandalf was in charge of.

The chill-out area was a place where people worn out on dance music could laze around somewhere comfortable during the festival.

He wanted us to make some kind of nice decoration for it.

"Something like a Japanese garden, for example..." Gandalf said, and he sounded very much like he wanted a Japanese garden made by actual Japanese people as part of the festival, so we decided to build a Japanese garden.

Not that either I-chan or I had set foot in a Japanese garden since our junior high school class trips, so we didn't have the faintest idea what to do, or how.

## The Wall of Time

Freed from absolute time, I had entered a relative axis of time, and with that, the speed of the music changed.

And the range of that change kept widening, faster and faster.

You could say I was simply wrecked and seeing things, but the hallucinations were not coming out of nowhere at random — they were an extension of the state of my own consciousness.

Now that I was inside that relative axis, the framework of the time I was living in began to waver.

Something I only worked out later: in the world outside, the hour just then was probably dusk.

Having slipped out of the framework of the sense of time, I wondered when, exactly, now was.

We had come to the party in the bright hours of the afternoon, but the sky showing through the gaps in the jungle had already turned to pitch-black night.

So it's night, I thought, and the instant I did, the night sky brightened before my eyes and came all the way back to full daylight.

So it was still daytime after all, I thought — and now the bright blue sky went black before my eyes and turned into a jungle party in the dark.

Absolute time no longer existed there; day or night was whatever my own senses made it.

## Time Running Backward

The absolute time that had stood outside me, unshakable, lost its power, and I went on into a relative time that stretched and shrank with my own senses.

This time my inner time began to run backward, and my sense of myself as a person regressed with it.

As the LSD went on building, the amplification of my consciousness pushed past its limit, to a stage where holding on to ordinary logical consciousness would have shattered it.

As a means of survival, my consciousness seems to have chosen, unconsciously, to turn itself into an infant.

Logical thought receded further and further, and what I moved into was a pure state of consciousness, like a baby's.

I lay down in the chai stall and looked up at my girlfriend I-chan with shining, innocent eyes.

I had turned into a baby wanting to be coddled, and I asked to lie with my head in her lap, and in the middle of that deafening, crazy party I found calm and peace.

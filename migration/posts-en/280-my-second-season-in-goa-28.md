## The Appointed Day

S came to our place on the date and at the time we had fixed.

Morning — the part of the day when your head is still clear.

I had been told to cut out meat a few days in advance, and I had not smoked cannabis since the day before.

A detox of body and consciousness is apparently a necessary part of the process.

The way S told it, when you smoke DMT you fly off into a world of hallucination in an instant, lose the ability to do anything at all, and collapse.

It runs about twenty minutes, and since you can do nothing at all for that whole time, the important thing is to get yourself ready to fall before you smoke.

And he told me to keep one thing firmly in mind: breathing in a full lungful and holding it there is what matters for the high.

## Getting Ready

S puffed on a cigarette and packed the burnt-out ash loosely into a pipe.

On top of it he set the DMT — a gooey substance, like earwax.

Heat turns DMT liquid, he said, so laying it on the cigarette ash lets you smoke every last bit of it.

I lay down on my side, and S held the pipe out and had me take it in my mouth.

That, it seemed, was the plan: once I had smoked it all, he would tip me straight over onto my back.

He asked if I was ready, and with my answer the session began.

## The DMT Session

I took deep breath after deep breath, the way he told me to.

After several of them, S put the pipe to my mouth and lit it.

Slowly, the way he had told me, I kept drawing it in.

A foul smell spread to fill my mouth — naphthalene, the mothballs you put in a chest of drawers.

After a while more of drawing, the instruction: "Hold your breath."

S kept repeating it — "Hold it in tight."

His voice drew away, little by little, to the far side of my consciousness.

The next thing I knew, I was letting the breath out slowly.

S saw it and laid me down on my back.

By then my consciousness had already begun heading for another dimension.

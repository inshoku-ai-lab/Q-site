## The Answer

Just getting a conversation with I-chan was hard, but I managed to find a moment and pressed her on what was going on inside her.

Her answer: she'd tell me when the time came.

That didn't satisfy me at all, but when someone tells you to wait, you have no choice but to wait.

So I went through the rest of the hut job frustrated.

## Hell

We'd been together two years, traveled the world side by side, and gotten through the good times and the hard ones together, and now here she was, working in the same place as me, and far from getting along, we couldn't hold a proper conversation, or even look each other in the eye.

On top of that, she never explained why, and everything was left hanging.

Whenever I tried to get close to her, she glared at me like a demon.

I was confused, and the pain felt like hell.

What saved me was the rest of the staff.

The work was busy, but working alongside so many people was fun, and I learned a lot.

## The End

Even those days of confusion and pain came to an end.

It was time to shut the big hut down completely.

We'd been closing the hut bit by bit, but just like at the other hut, the last few days had no guests at all, and we moved on to the final big cleanup.

That was when I-chan asked to talk.

It turned out she hadn't been too busy to talk all this time; she hadn't wanted to, and had been using the work as an excuse to avoid me.

According to I-chan, spending that time apart from me had meant living on her own, standing on her own two feet, day after day.

She'd felt the joy of being independent, and so she didn't want to go back to being a couple.

The time she'd spent with me had been fun in a lot of ways, but she'd never been able to feel fully independent.

From now on, she wanted us to break up and go our separate ways.

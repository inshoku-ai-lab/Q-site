## Seeing F Again

Gandalf lived out in the countryside, so we decided to meet up with F in town first and then go see Gandalf together.

Even at the oasis, F had given off a big-city air, and when we visited his place, sure enough, the impression held.

After so long away from city life, I was a little surprised by how urban it all was, and by how completely F fit into it.

## A City Drink

He lived with a young girlfriend who'd dyed her hair blond, and he drank espresso mixed with a strong, sweet liquor.

In a way, it was very stylish, with something distinctly urban about it.

But neither of us drank alcohol or coffee, so the idea of drinking the two together took us aback.

My honest reaction was: "That can't be good for you..."

After living as one with nature at the oasis, it felt pretty jarring.

## Exhaust Fumes

Once we'd rested up a bit at F's place, Gandalf came to pick us up.

We rode around Tangier in a taxi for a while, then headed out to the countryside where Gandalf lived.

Gandalf watched the traffic, and whenever he spotted a car pouring out clouds of exhaust, he'd call it a crime against the Earth and get angry.

Not just in Morocco but in any so-called Third World country, the laws and regulations might as well not exist, and cars are driven until they fall apart completely.

Breaking down once or twice doesn't even count as breaking down; they get fixed dozens of times and keep on running.

By the later chapters of these comeback stories, the exhaust and the engine noise alike are screaming.

Through Gandalf's anger, I slowly got used to thinking about the Earth's environment.

At first I didn't really get Gandalf's Earth consciousness, and thought of it as little more than "kind of extreme."

But as time went on, his way of thinking was seeping into me.

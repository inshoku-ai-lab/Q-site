## England

The other European country I was interested in was England.

English is a skill a traveler cannot do without, and the best way to get better at it is to live in an English-speaking country — plenty of Japanese travelers with good English said the same thing.

In the world of travelers, where people gather from everywhere on earth, not being able to speak English left you out of the conversation entirely.

About a year and a half on the road had gotten me to broken English at the level of bare survival, but bare survival was exactly where it stopped, and if anything I had gone out of my way to avoid English.

And of course, England meant the cutting edge of music and culture.

For someone aiming at a life as a musician, it was an attractive place.

I could study English, and I could get close to music at its cutting edge.

Those two alone were enough to make me want to go, and then came the perfect push.

## N

N, who I hung around with the whole time in Goa last year, and T, who I had been hanging around with through Goa last year, Vashisht, and Goa again this year, were best friends, and the two of them had shared a house in London for about three years.

I had heard all kinds of entertaining stories from those days, so I had been picturing it myself — living in London could be nice, too.

Just then, word came that N had made her way back to London a few months earlier and started living there again.

And she gave me a generous invitation: come and visit anytime.

On top of that, she told me she might even have a connection for part-time work.

It felt like every circumstance had fallen perfectly into place and was inviting me to England.

English, music, a close friend, that friend's support, work, and so on — the stage could not have been better set for my first step into Europe.

## Talking It Over

When I told my girlfriend I-chan that I was thinking about going to England, she said yes on the spot.

To begin with, I-chan had come to India this time not so much to travel as to train as an actress.

That dream, unfortunately, never came to anything and fell apart, but she still meant to go back to Japan eventually and make her way as a stage actress.

Until then, she said, she wanted to treasure the time she had with me, her boyfriend.

So where we moved next did not seem to be something she was attached to one way or the other.

Thanks to I-chan and that big heart of hers, our next destination was decided: London, England.

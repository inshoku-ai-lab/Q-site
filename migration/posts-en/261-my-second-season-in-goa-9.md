## How to Express Discontent

It was my first time being in a relationship, and I had hardly ever socialized with anyone to begin with.

As a small child I was quiet and shy, the kid who got bullied, and later, living alone in Osaka, I went headlong into full geekdom, so I had come this far without ever really learning how to deal with people.

In that state, and in a relationship inside an environment this extreme, I had no good way to put my own discontent into words.

Not one bit of it was I-chan's fault, and still I found her unbelievably irritating.

And instead of expressing that feeling, I went on the offensive with the same method I had used as a child: the tactic of unconscious illness.

## Headaches

Faced with an environment that was more than I could handle, I had headaches.

It wasn't faked — the pain was real.

And with LSD driving my thinking into overdrive, I worried and stressed far more than the situation called for, which made the headaches worse.

And for me, with no way to express my discontent to I-chan, the headaches were a convenient tool.

I used them as a reason to keep psychological distance from I-chan, and as an excuse for being in a bad mood.

At twenty-one I had never once spoken my real discontent to anyone close to me, and I didn't even know whether it was something a person was supposed to express.

## Discontent

Sour as I was, I tried my hardest to act cheerful.

I tried to enjoy this dud of a Goa season to the fullest, and there really were plenty of good moments, but underneath everything lay my discontent with I-chan, the feeling that she was uncool.

What felt uncool to me was I-chan's ability to fit into society.

She had grown up in the second-largest satellite city of Japan's second-largest city, the second daughter of a middle-class family, and that safe, unobjectionable position of hers made me angry.

It made me angry that she had worked as an office lady and gotten along with her boss, and it made me angry that she knew how to flatter her superiors just enough to get by.

All of it was nothing but a false charge, an absurd piece of lashing out, and still I laid the blame on her, one-sided, for reasons entirely my own.

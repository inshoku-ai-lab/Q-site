# Believe It or Not, That's Up to You

Riding the flow in your travels for years on end, you start to acquire a strange power.

Magnetism, or ki, or vibration, or frequency — some strange power, whatever you want to call it.

People who carry this kind of power seem to be made to find one another naturally, and so all sorts of strange encounters happen.

![A manga panel of a face in close-up, captioned in Japanese with the idea that people who carry the same power are drawn to one another](/images/wp/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/002stand.jpg)

Among them are a few people with unusual abilities — reading past lives and the Akashic records, painting pictures of souls, holding conversations with guide spirits or with individual souls, and so on.

Basically I don't put much faith in this sort of thing, but since the people said to have these abilities are personal friends of mine, it is easy enough for me to trust them.

What raised my trust in particular was that separate people painted the picture of my soul and each one came out looking just like the rest.

Of course I don't think every last detail of it lines up with the facts, but believing seems more fun, so I have decided to believe.

# The Guide Spirit

![An illustration of a figure walking away up a glowing golden path into swirling blue light, a large hairless dog padding along it in the foreground](/images/wp/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/002guide-500x400.jpg)

Put the separate accounts of my previous lives together and they go something like this.

A tremendously powerful guide spirit — a guardian spirit? — is attached to me, and my soul is apparently repeating its incarnations with a purpose.

The strength of that guide spirit is enough to command an entire galaxy, and it is apparently not compulsion by force but a natural kind of leadership, arising from the respect that those around it hold for my wisdom.

Carrying a guide spirit like that, I have moved from past life to past life, but this time is apparently the last life in this form.

Whether it is the physical dimension that is ending, or the Earth, or something else, I don't know, but the form is apparently going to change.

Where to next, I wonder.

# Past Lives

Everyone carries all sorts of individual traits of the soul, and my past lives apparently went something like this.

Beating drums in Africa.

Training alone in the jungle.

Serving as a high priest in the Himalayas.

There was a life born into a wealthy Russian household and spent painting from beginning to end.

Fighting as a guerrilla soldier in the Cuban Revolution.

Then there was a life sending messages from this planet out toward other planets.

And separate from that one, there was also a life sending messages from other planets toward this one.

# The Companions of the Deserted Island

Among all those past lives, the interesting story is this one.

Long, long ago, I received a revelation ringing inside my head, and half-doubting it, I did what the revelation said and headed for a certain deserted island.

It was an age without civilization as it is now, and reaching that deserted island required considerable hardship, but somehow I arrived.

When I arrived on the island I found other people there, and they said they had each received a revelation ringing in their heads too and had come from all sorts of regions.

Among them were people who said their ship had been wrecked in a storm and they had barely made it there alive.

We celebrated our miraculous, fated encounter with one another heartily.

Life on that island was primitive and harsh, far from convenient, but we did not much mind the inconvenience and enjoyed the primitive island life.

Then one day we took to amusing ourselves by drawing enormous pictograms with stones.

At one point I started saying, "These pictograms we draw as a group are not just pictograms, they are an act with some deep cosmic meaning," and taking that as the cue, everyone on the island began throwing themselves into the work.

This past life may be connected to the life sending messages from this planet out toward other planets, or it may have something to do with the founding of some civilization or organization.

The details are unknown.

These companions of the deserted island I have met again, by fate, through my travels in this life.

They are the companions I played and traveled with all over the world — caravanning together through Central America, building a community together in Costa Rica, driving through Europe and North America, living together deep in the mountains of Mexico.

I am doing things in this life that resemble each of my own past lives, and this life has come to feel like the culmination of them all.

Whether it is a ceremony of parting with this planet, or one last round of play, or whether I am going to use this experience to do something...

# Conception

With all of that behind me, I came into this body to live my last life.

My soul apparently wanted, no matter what, to be born in this era and this place.

But there was no suitable vacant body, and while that was holding things up, my mother apparently agreed reluctantly to conceive me.

So you did it reluctantly, then — that is where I want to put in a retort, but on top of conceiving me she raised me, which is truly something to be grateful for.

# Birth

On a day at the height of summer in the late 1970s, at exactly noon, I was apparently born measuring exactly fifty centimeters (nearly twenty inches) and weighing exactly three thousand grams (six and a half pounds).

Since it comes from my mother, who is from Kansai, the story may be padded somewhat, but I like the roundness of the numbers.

As a departure point for a last life, it could not be better.

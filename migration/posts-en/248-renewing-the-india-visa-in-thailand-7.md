## Renewing My Passport

My passport had come up for renewal, so I decided this was the moment to take care of it.

It had been issued when I was seventeen, and the photo still held the boyish innocence of someone who had just walked out of high school; the gap between that and the traveler I was trying to become was so wide that I wanted it changed as soon as possible.

On top of that, since I meant to go on traveling, a ten-year passport seemed more committed, and cooler, than a five-year one.

And renewing it in Thailand rather than back in Japan seemed more international, and cooler still.

The motives were childish, but the renewal went through without trouble, and I set about arranging a flight to India.

Back then there was no such thing as online booking, and the normal way was to buy your ticket at a travel agency with an actual storefront.

Going on information picked up through traveler connections, I bought a dirt-cheap ticket to Delhi at a Sikh-run travel agency down a back street.

Agencies like that, the ones other travelers steer you to, can run about twenty percent below the usual price, so whether you know about them or not makes a big difference.

## To India

With every preparation made, I boarded the plane for Delhi.

I had traveled by plane enough times by then that none of it surprised me anymore, but air travel still feels good.

And more than anything else, I was glad to be going back to India.

India has some particular energy swirling through it that is hard to put into words, and on a certain kind of person it has a strange effect — just being in India is enough to make everything feel complete.

I was one of them: even a vacation with my best friends on a southern Thai island had left something in me unsatisfied, and going to India settled it.

## Delhi

Delhi, where I had been only a short while before, had not changed in the slightest: the din, the beggars, the stray dogs, and the cows still ruled the streets.

My plan had been to head straight south for Goa once I got to Delhi, but I thought better of it and decided to go back up to Vashisht, the village in the Himalayas, take my time in the hot springs, and make for Goa after that.

I was riding the momentum of the road, so I booked a bus for the next day and set out for the Himalayas.

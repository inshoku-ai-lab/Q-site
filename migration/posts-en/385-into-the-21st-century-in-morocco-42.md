## The Ruins

We walked through the darkness toward the light and sound.

As the dance floor drew closer, I could make out something behind the stage that looked like ruins, ancient or maybe just derelict, lit up by spotlights.

With the added strangeness of the festival itself, a faint air of mystery hung over everything.

In a few more hours the twentieth century would end and a new one would begin, and we were standing right on that threshold.

Morocco, the Sahara, an oasis, an island, a festival, and a new century.

All kinds of things ran through my head, and excitement surged through me.

## The Twenty-First Century

We reached the dance floor and melted into the sound and light.

The whole floor moved as one, swaying to the beat.

Every one of us had come all the way to the Moroccan desert hoping to dance across this moment into the twenty-first century with the biggest smiles we had.

The excitement, the emotion, and the joy were shared by all of us.

Right as the date, the year, and the century were about to change, the DJ stopped the music.

The countdown to that moment began.

Ten, nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two, one...

At zero, fireworks shot into the sky, and the whole floor was swept up in light, sound, and emotion.

Just before the frenzy died down, the DJ slipped a beat back in.

Beautifully done. Perfect timing.

The frenzy on the floor never cooled; it poured straight into wild dancing.

## Sunrise

Everyone was swept up in the excitement, dancing as if nothing else existed.

It was like a kind of mass hypnosis, and the dancers were deliberately trying to fall under it.

Any sense of time was already gone; rapture, oneness, and contentment ruled.

The next time I came back to myself, the sun was rising.

As the sky grew lighter, the faces of the diehards came into view, smeared with sweat and dust.

Every one of them was filthy beyond words, and at the same time shining with smiles.

Everyone there was glad to be greeting this moment in this place, and we could all feel, on a gut level, that everyone else felt the same.

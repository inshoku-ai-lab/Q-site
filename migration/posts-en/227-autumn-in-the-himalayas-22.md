## The Cream Experience

The man had gone up the mountain himself, kneaded the finest cream, handed it around for free without taking any money, and once the full sequence of ritual had been done, we all smoked it together.

It was the ultimate Indian cannabis experience, and nothing could top it.

The man who lit it first took a few pulls without letting the smoke into his lungs, working the fire through the whole load packed in the chillum.

By the time it came around to me, it was drawing perfectly.

Following the ritual myself, I took the chillum carefully, my left hand supporting my right.

I drew lightly at first to warm it, then pulled the smoke deep into my lungs, held it there, and let it out through my nose.

The smoke was gentle on the throat, sliding into my body like a caress.

Along with the refined texture of it, I felt the breath of Shiva, the kind that takes your consciousness over to the other side in a single instant.

## Another Dimension

That one hit settled everything.

My mind was carried off into another dimension, and unable even to stay sitting up, I slipped out of the chillum circle and lay down.

Strictly speaking that was a breach of etiquette, but in that state there was nothing to be done about it.

A twenty-one-year-old brat gets cut some slack.

The floating feeling the cannabis gave me was unlike anything I had known, and I drifted through a cosmic world in another dimension.

The sensation made me feel the unreality of the real world in my own body, and the fixed ideas I had carried until then seemed to come undone.

I understood why the sadhus of Shiva smoke a chillum, and I understood how much power there is in smoking it through the ritual.

## Passing Out

My awareness kept sliding away until I blacked out.

The next time I opened my eyes, hours had gone by and everyone was heading back down to the village below.

He wouldn't part with the cream he had made himself, but what he had bought from the villagers he was willing to sell, so I decided to buy some of that and take it with me.

The price was about five times what the ordinary stuff cost, but for that quality it was almost cheap.

I came away with the finest from villages all over, a collection to keep and to smoke on special occasions with the friends who mattered most.

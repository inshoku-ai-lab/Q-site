## The Last Days

We had said our goodbyes to Gandalf and F, and we were getting ready to leave Morocco.

After this, I would go live in London, and I-chan would pursue theater back in Osaka.

We were leaving Morocco behind, and our relationship was about to go on indefinite hold.

Our time traveling around Morocco had been a lot of fun, and now it was ending.

We had dragged our stay out as long as we could, and our visas had already been expired for two weeks.

Our flight was booked, and it was only a few days away.

## Leaving the Country

We went through customs at the port in Tangier and boarded the ship.

The officer looked at our passports and asked about the expired visas, but once we said we'd been sick and unable to travel, we were let straight through.

I'd been worried about what would come of staying illegally on an expired visa, but in the end it was no big deal.

After a few hours at sea, the ferry made it safely to the Spanish mainland.

From there, we headed straight for the airport in Málaga.

## Reunion

And here came a surprise reunion.

C was right there, inside the Málaga airport.

He was so neatly dressed you'd never have pictured it back in the desert.

He said he was on his way to France.

The plan, apparently, was to work in France for a while, then come back to Morocco and help Gandalf.

It sounded like Gandalf planned to keep launching all kinds of ecology projects.

I was curious to see what would come of them.

C told us he had crossed into the Spanish territory of Ceuta, in the northeast of Morocco, the day before we reached Tangier, and come over to the Spanish mainland from there.

People who stayed in Morocco long-term renewed their visas by crossing the border at Ceuta once every three months, leaving a record that they'd been to Spain.

Every country has some easy trick for renewing a visa, and in Morocco, Ceuta had become something of a loophole.

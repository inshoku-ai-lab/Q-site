## Growing Up

From the moment I took twenty-four times the normal dose of LSD, the hours kept passing and I never slept a wink.

The whole way through, the high never let up.

The baby's sense of self I had during the party had been at zero; by the time I went to sleep it had grown to about a year and a half.

In the end I was awake about forty hours that day before I slept.

The LSD was still going strong, though, so I woke up after about five hours.

Awake, I did nothing in particular and just spaced out.

By then my sense of self was up to around three years old.

When my girlfriend I-chan got up, I told her, in the voice of a three-year-old, "Me, I'm about three right now."

How she might take that never once entered my head.

She told me later that she had been close to panic, with no idea what to do about the whole outrageous situation.

## Running Its Course

The days went on, and as they passed the LSD thinned out and my sense of self grew a little at a time.

Time seemed to move about three years for every day that went by.

So I went through the growth of the self in order, from baby up to the twenty-one-year-old I actually was.

For that whole week, the high never let up.

Day after day — today six, today ten, today fifteen — I lived the growth of the self through again.

## Purification

I think this was, in a way, a ritual of rebirth.

In only twenty-one years of life I had already been through a few things that felt like being reborn, but this time it was rebirth itself, a second birth and nothing less.

I think it purified the painful years of my childhood.

At the very least, in that baby state there was no negativity in me at all.

Put the other way around, I think the only way to come through a dose of LSD that size was to drop every attachment, shake off the negativity, switch off thought, and become a baby.

And so, by passing through the ritual of a second birth, I came through the biggest event of my life over the course of a week.

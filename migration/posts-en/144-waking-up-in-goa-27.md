## Sunrise

We just kept dancing, on and on, and morning came while the excitement was still running high.

The sea changed color as the sun climbed, and the sight of it stuck with me.

The smiling faces of the people dancing around me jumped into view, and the moment our eyes met, a smile broke out on my own face without my deciding on it.

Goa Gil, the DJ, timed it for exactly that moment and pushed the party up another level.

The high we were already on pulled a bigger one in behind it, and the whole thing burst open into joy.

## Hours on End

Hours of dancing did not bore us — if anything we went further and further in, urged on by the tracks that kept rolling out one after another.

The runner's high that comes from dancing for hours was something I had already met at earlier parties, but this one took me to a dimension past even that.

Dancing on past the limit of what my body had left brought on a state you could fairly call a trance.

My mind went hazy and my senses went sharp at the same time, my body and the music became one thing, and it stopped being me doing the dancing — the sound took hold of the body, or the music possessed it.

Dancing inside that feeling, I understood what Goa Gil was trying to say through his DJing.

For him, a Goa trance party was a way to widen perception through an experience out of another dimension, and to bring people's consciousness alive through experience from a different angle.

He DJed as a shaman, the one who leads the way.

## Sunset

The party just kept going after the sun came up.

Nobody flinched in the direct sunlight; everyone kept dancing with everything they had.

Nobody bothered with food.

Water was the only thing anyone took in, drunk by the bucketful, and every bit of energy went into the dancing.

Sunset arrived in the middle of a staggering frenzy and a staggering sense of being one body.

The blood-red sun going down into the Arabian Sea was beautiful.

And right as it went down, he put on the track he had been saving, and the whole thing lifted one more time.

With the sun gone and the party at its absolute peak, it ended.

A huge wave of applause went up, and voices calling out their thanks.

## The Trance

Every one of us had been through a miraculous trance, and every one of us was grateful for the luck of having been given it.

Christmas in Goa, the thing I had only ever heard about in rumors, turned out to be on a level the rumors could not touch.

That one night was so overwhelmingly positive, so full of life force, that it felt as though every negative thought I had ever carried had been settled and cleared away.

Everyone understood by instinct that once you go through something like this even once, your life has no choice but to change, and there is no going back to the world you came from.

That understanding held a fear inside it, as if the ground had suddenly gone out from under you, and at the same time an exhilaration, as if a new horizon of freedom had opened up.

Goa's Christmas party had set us free.

## The Dangerous Face

The village had a kind face, and it had a dangerous one too.

Practically every village in the valley was a cannabis-farming village, and almost everyone in them made their income from cannabis in one form or another.

They grew the crop, pulled out the resin, worked it into a form that was easy to handle, carried it down from the villages deep in the mountains to the villages along the valley, sold it, moved it on across India, and from there out into the world.

Alongside that drug business there was the work of handling the travelers who came through — running the guesthouses, serving the food, arranging trips and the like.

At a glance none of it looked especially dangerous, but the dangerous part was "carried it down from the villages deep in the mountains to the villages along the valley."

Travelers who wanted the top grade cheap would climb for days to reach the villages deep in the mountains and buy their hash there.

With a great deal of cash hidden inside their clothes...

## Incidents

That cash was what the bandits were after, and the police, and the farmers, and the competition.

On the way up, a fat roll of money; on the way back, a load of hash.

By the standard of an average local income, either one was worth years of earnings.

Carry that kind of money along a mountain road and the best you could expect was a threat; the worst was a shove off the path into the valley below.

This part of the mountains was effectively lawless, so a single push into the ravine was enough to make a crime perfect.

In the restaurants around the area you saw missing-person posters up all the time, and everyone knew the person on them was not coming back.

They had stepped into the part of it no one was supposed to step into, and what they got was what they had coming.

## Reckless Courage

And still the travelers set out for the deep mountains, after the finest hash there was.

The people traveling this part of the world had been piling adventure on adventure for years, and in many of them fear had gone numb, so off they went further into the mountains, looking for more.

Come back from a successful buy and that alone could bring in enough money to spend about a year in India living for the fun of it — the finest hash, a pile of cash, a war story, and a name.

Among the cannabis-loving travelers of India, carrying good cannabis meant your status went straight up, and on the day you got hold of the very best hash, you shot straight to stardom.

It was ridiculous, but that was how much good cannabis was worth.

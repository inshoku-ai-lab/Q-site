## I-chan's Performance

While I was spending my days going nowhere at a chain restaurant, my girlfriend I-chan was throwing herself into rehearsals with her theater company, shining a little brighter every day.

In the final week before the performance, the rehearsals grew even more intense, and her days were packed.

I'd never been part of that kind of group effort, never worked hard at rehearsing anything, so I felt a vague envy watching her.

Her rehearsals went on, and about a month later came the long-awaited performance.

I went to a theater in the city of Osaka and took my seat.

I-chan had told me not to come backstage until the performance was over.

She wanted to hold on to the emotion, it seemed.

## The Acting

The stage went dark, and the performance began.

I'm sorry to say that, looking back now, I can't clearly remember what the play was about.

I think it portrayed the inner lives of human beings through all sorts of staging techniques, a slightly niche, experimental piece.

Her acting was full of heat, and she had more presence than the other actors.

Maybe I was biased because she was my girlfriend, but she was something special.

I-chan seemed confident in her talent as an actress, and she'd once told me that when she danced, she had to hold herself back, or the other actors' presence would disappear. Watching her act, I understood what she'd meant.

On stage, she wore thick, heavily layered makeup that made her already strong features stand out even more.

Feeling what the character feels, standing where the character stands, and expressing things from there.

I have no talent like that, so watching I-chan put hers to use moved me.

Seeing a side of her completely different from the one I knew from the world of travel, I felt as if I'd slipped into some other world, one that set me on edge.

The play ended with the curtain falling to a moving female vocal, and the performance closed to thunderous applause.

I-chan was busy after the show too, so I just said a quick hello and headed home, dumbfounded by a completely unknown world, an experience I'd never had, and a side of her I had never seen before.

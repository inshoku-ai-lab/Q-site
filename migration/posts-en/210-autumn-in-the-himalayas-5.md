## The Party Baba

In India, baba is the word for an older man; sometimes it points to men in general, used much the way you would address someone as mister in English.

Baba is also used for sadhus, the wandering monks.

When it refers to a sadhu, the word usually comes attached to a sect within Hinduism; the sadhus of Shiva are the common ones, but there are also Kali babas, wrapped in black, with something devil-worshipping about them.

And then there are the ones called party babas, who give themselves over to the pleasures of the parties in Goa and Manali and run with the hippie travelers.

They run the whole range, top to bottom: there are babas whose consciousness has been woken by LSD, who have thrown off worldly desire and opened their spirituality, and who are respected by the travelers, and there are party babas who have drowned in the carnal side of party culture and lost the trust of everyone around them.

The air around the top and the bottom is obviously different, so once you have traveled a while you can tell them apart immediately, but young people just starting out get taken in by how fierce the men look and end up as prey.

Prey may be too strong a word — they are not villains on that scale — but you get hit up for meals at restaurants, or sold hash at inflated prices.

One of those babas was at this indoor party as well.

I had known him by sight from my earlier time in Goa and had kept my distance because of the bad air about him, but there was no way to stop him from coming to a party anyone was free to walk into.

If anything, the reverse was true: nobody had broken out of the ordinary world with as much force as he had, and that kind of presence can be good spice at a party.

## F, the Jock Who Was Still a Kid

At the indoor party we took the LSD we had set aside in advance.

For my new friends B and F, this was the first time either of them had taken it.

I knew how my own body worked, so I took my usual small dose and held to the line right at the edge of my limit without crossing it, and enjoyed myself; my jock friends treated it like a drinking party and started off with a whole tab of paper acid each.

That was the standard single dose, but in Manali the travelers brought LSD in straight from Europe, so it was fresh, and much of it was stronger than usual.

Taking a whole tab your first time, on top of that, was a serious risk.

But of course, being jocks, none of that meant anything to them.

They were fearless, and risk was welcome.

## A Dud

For me, I-chan, and Y, the Christmas party was a dud.

A-san and D, on the other hand, were having a great time.

The setup itself was faultless — huge speakers, a DJ who knew exactly what he was doing — but the bizarre, dangerous adventure I had come for, the kind that throws you into another dimension, wasn't there.

Y and I both knew what Goa had been the year before, so we could not help comparing, and neither of us could enjoy it all the way.

At the same time we kept picturing the party in South Africa, where everyone had to be having the time of their lives right about then, and it left us with a vague sense that we had lost, and we could never quite get swept up in it.

Without quite realizing it, we felt like we had become the uncool travelers, and that was galling, and it was embarrassing.

Looking back now it is a ridiculous thing to have cared about, but at the time nothing mattered more to me than riding the absolute cutting edge of the times.

And the childish part of me was about to aim all of that frustration at I-chan, my girlfriend and my partner.

## Blamed for Nothing

From I-chan's side it was nothing but a nuisance, but I found it uncool that she wasn't getting all the way gone on LSD, and it left me dissatisfied.

I-chan was set on becoming an actress, and she looked the part: beautiful, good figure, bright personality, and on top of that the adventurous streak to be traveling India at twenty-one.

She was smart as well, and thoughtful in the small ways — by any measure a wonderful woman.

What she did not have was the thing I was after: artistic talent that crossed the line, a hunger for worlds she had never seen, the hippie cool of someone who had stepped outside of society.

Instead of being glad for what was there, I was irritated by what was not, and I let the dissatisfaction build.

I understood perfectly well that none of it was I-chan's fault, and I knew I had no business taking it out on her.

Even so, my mood stayed low, and that gloom moved in step with Y's work on the film and with the mood of Goa as a whole.

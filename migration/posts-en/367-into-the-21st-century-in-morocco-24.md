## The Hotel

The week or so before the festival went by in a blur.

More and more travelers kept arriving at the hotel; every room filled up, and the hotel was bringing out makeshift rooms and spare bedding to cope.

They seemed to have raised their rates sharply, too.

We had paid up front for our whole stay at a rock-bottom 300 yen a day — about two and a half dollars — so the hotel people may well have resented us.

## Larger Than Life

Seasoned travelers had gathered at the hotel from all over the world, but the ones giving off the strongest aura were a pair, D and E.

Both were tall, powerfully built musicians; D had dreadlocks down to his waist, and E wore his hair so short it was nearly shaved.

D carried around an enormous djembe every bit as big as he was, and E carried around a didgeridoo every bit as big as he was.

The two of them would join in the jam sessions everyone was having, but their instruments were nothing like the size of what travelers usually carry, and the sheer force of the sound overwhelmed everything around them.

They were superb players, too; the moment they started, the air around them changed completely.

Apparently they lived in a squat house in Switzerland and paid no rent.

Their food came from dumpster diving.

## His Way of Traveling

D had been living on the road, traveling the world, for ten years or so, it seemed, and he knew all kinds of useful things.

His know-how for staying on the road was impressive — the squat and the dumpsters were part of it — but he also had a head for business.

He bought top-quality quartz cheaply deep in the Indian Himalayas — India, the cheapest country in the world — and sold it to his friends in Switzerland, the most expensive country in the world.

He had the charismatic aura of someone who has been traveling for years, so plenty of people wanted to buy quartz from him.

He was selling in an expensive country while his living costs were close to zero, so every bit of the profit could go into his travels.

Once a year he went from India to Switzerland, and he spent the rest of his time traveling around the world.

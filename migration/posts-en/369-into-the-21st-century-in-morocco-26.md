## Christmas

The year was drawing to a close, and Christmas Day came around.

D and E and their friends, a crew of Swiss travelers, had invited us to a Christmas party.

Their musical talent and their experience on the road made them fascinating company, but what set them apart even more was the drugs they carried.

I had brought hash with me from Chaouen, and word had gone around among the travelers that mine was top grade, so people treated me with a certain respect.

But what D had brought was in another league.

Before Morocco he had apparently been in India, and he had brought the very finest hash from there.

## Malana Cream

What he had wasn't just good-quality cannabis. It was Malana cream, a class apart even among the top grades, the ultimate, the finest in the world.

The Parvati Valley, up in the Indian Himalayas, is famous for the finest cannabis there is, and inside that valley lies a kingdom called Malana.

It isn't an official nation, the kind the United Nations would recognize; on paper it's just a village deep in the mountains, but in practice it exists as a kingdom.

The village can only be reached by walking steep mountain trails for about two days, and because it's so remote, the government and the police have no hold over it.

There is a king, apparently, and the villagers live in his service.

The kingdom's only income is hash, pure and simple, and they grow their own food.

It has existed since the ancient days of Indian history, crossbreeding its cannabis down through the ages to create the ultimate plant, and its master hash makers knead the resin into shape.

Stuff that good never turns up on the ordinary market; the only ways to get it are to walk into the mountains yourself and buy it there, or to get it from a master traveler like D.

I had heard the legend of Malana cream while I was traveling in India, but I had never come across the real thing.

And now here it was, right in front of me.

I was deeply moved.

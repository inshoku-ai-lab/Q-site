## The End

Our communal life was drawing to a close.

None of us had ever really understood what this communal life meant in the first place, but at the same time, none of us thought it would go on forever.

The breaking point came from Gandalf.

He had run out of money.

I doubt he had actually burned through everything he owned, but I think the money he was willing to spend feeding everyone for free had run dry.

More than that, I suspect he decided to end our communal life as a way of getting rid of R.

Toward the end, R had become a thorn in Gandalf's side.

It was as if whatever was eating at R had infected Gandalf too.

## The Announcement

The announcement came out of nowhere.

Gandalf said:

"The rent on the hut is up for renewal in three days, but I don't have the money, so I'd like to bring this way of living to an end."

On top of that, his visa was about to expire. He had overstayed several times in the past and could never do it again, so he absolutely had to leave the country, at least for a while.

With all of these things piling up, there was no way to keep this life going.

I think the timing was right for everyone.

## Camping

But I-chan and I still hadn't had enough.

So we decided that even after the communal life ended, we would camp by the lake for a few more days.

Word was that the stream running past the hut tumbled over a stepped, rocky stretch on its way into the lake, turning into something like a waterfall.

The plan was to spend our days camping around that waterfall.

The one who jumped at the idea was Y.

He has a knack for climbing aboard anything that sounds fun.

We agreed that a camping trip with just us Japanese sounded like fun too, and the plan came together on the spot.

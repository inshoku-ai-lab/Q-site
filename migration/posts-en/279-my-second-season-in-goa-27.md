## DMT

Word was going around about a drug called DMT.

I had first heard about it six months or so earlier, but it never got past being a rumor. Whether the stuff even existed was doubtful, and I never thought a chance to actually take it would come along.

The rumor said it was a hallucinogen about ten times stronger than LSD, one that brought on outrageous hallucinations.

From what people said, dolphins would appear and swim the ocean with you, or you would have sex with aliens, or you would travel to a dimension of beautiful geometric patterns.

What I learned a few years later is that DMT is dimethyltryptamine, the same chemical the brain releases when a person is dying — the substance that produces the near-death experience.

Going through a simulated death lets you brush up against a feeling something like enlightenment.

Ayahuasca, the drink South American shamans use, contains a lot of DMT, and it is used in spiritual practice.

In Goa in '99 nobody was talking about it, but once 2000 came around the rumor spread all at once.

One reason was that a Japanese traveler had gotten hold of a large quantity from somewhere and was going around running DMT sessions.

He understood the divinity DMT carries, and he gave sessions only to people who seriously wanted to explore the world of consciousness.

## The Meeting

Then one day, through some odd turn of events, I got an introduction to S, the traveler everyone was talking about.

I had heard the rumors, but DMT was something far out of reach, and I never imagined the day would come when I would try it myself.

But now that I had actually met S, there was no question of not asking him for a session.

When I asked, he wanted to know how many times I had done LSD.

Around fifty, I told him, and that satisfied him, and after sizing up the kind of person I was, he gave me permission to receive a session.

Going by the age range of the people he gave sessions to, at twenty-one I was apparently young by a wide margin.

We fixed a date and time to meet again, and it was settled: he would come to my place and run the session there.

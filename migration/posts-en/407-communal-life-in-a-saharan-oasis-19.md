## Sunrise

The magical full moon eclipse party, which had been an absolute blast, was drawing to a close.

The night was turning pale, and it was nearly time for the sun to come up.

The eastern sky began to brighten while the full moon sank toward the west.

But before it could reach the horizon, the moon faded into the light of dawn.

Right after that, the sun began to rise over the horizon.

It was unreal. Way too beautiful.

The full moon, the eclipse, the sunrise: every last thing was perfect, all of it held in a flawless harmony of beauty.

## Farewell

Once the sun was up, the summer heat arrived in no time.

Getting ready for summer, as we put it, we peeled off every layer we had on, stripped down to T-shirts and shorts, and braced ourselves for the scorching season.

As the morning sun grew fiercer, the moment came for D and E to set off.

Right on cue, the taxi they had booked pulled up.

They were heading to the airport, they said, and from there to India.

Part of me was rooting for them, part of me envied them, part of me wanted to go along, and part of me was sad to see them go.

We sent them off with the biggest smiles we had.

Seeing someone off leaves a little sadness behind.

And then comes the swell of hope for what lies ahead.

I felt as though I'd caught a glimpse of what it really means to be a traveler.

## Wrapping Up

The sun was up and starting to show its true, savage face.

It was far too hot to keep the party going.

We stowed all our things inside the hut, and that was the end of our full moon revels.

But the hut was so comfortable that we drifted straight into a breakfast party.

I-chan and I couldn't spend the daytime in our tent either, so we lazed around inside the hut.

Living with friends I felt so at home with was full of excitement, and yet there was something about it that set my mind at ease.

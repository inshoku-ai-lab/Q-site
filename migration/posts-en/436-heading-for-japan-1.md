## Back in Japan

The flight home went without a hitch, and I landed at Kansai Airport.

Back after a year and seven months away, something about Japan felt off.

It all looked familiar, and there was a pull of nostalgia, but at the same time I felt the alienation of an outsider, as if I no longer belonged there.

What I'd been through on the road was on another plane entirely, and there was no way to share what it had felt like.

With all that unease and alienation, I wasn't simply happy to be home, but even so, Japan after so long still felt exciting.

## Home to My Mother's

I changed trains a few times and headed for my family home, where my mother lived.

The house isn't far from Kansai Airport, so within an hour or two I was there.

Seeing my mother again after so long brought back old feelings, but there was a little tension too, since the two of us would have to live together in a cramped space.

Normally my mother and my grandfather would both have been at home, but my grandfather was in the hospital.

Seeing him before he died was the main reason I'd come back.

For now his condition was stable, but no one knew when it might take a turn for the worse, so he was staying in the hospital.

He was in for cancer, but at seventy-nine, the name of the disease hardly mattered.

His body was simply wearing out with age.

## My Grandfather

There was no telling when his condition might change, so the very next day I went to the hospital.

For now, I just wanted to see him.

I went to visit him at the big hospital in our neighborhood.

Apparently no one had told him that the doctors had diagnosed cancer.

Personally, I didn't like lying at a time like this, but my mother and my aunt were the ones taking care of him, so I had no right to complain.

My grandfather had wasted away since I'd last seen him, and there was a smell of death about him.

Anyone could tell at a glance, by instinct, that he didn't have long.

On the surface, everyone said things like "You'll be better soon," but everyone could sense that his time was coming.

He probably understood it himself.

But he played his part in the drama too, keeping up the pretense that he'd be better soon.

Doing that probably kept things running smoothly in the family.

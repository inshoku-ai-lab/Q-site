## The New Century

I rang in the new century in the best possible mood.

To me it was a sign that a hundred fantastic years lay ahead, and that was what I had come all the way to the Sahara for.

The sunrise came and went, the sun climbed, and it got too hot to stay out under it for long, so I headed for the tent where our friends were gathered.

A-kun, who had been in bad shape the night before, seemed to have settled down in the new century; he was lying in a corner with a smile on his face.

For now, it looked as if everything had worked itself out.

The festival went on until the night of January 2, and we had the time of our lives.

Enjoying the music, dancing hard, laughing our heads off with friends.

Good times don't make for much of a story once you write them down.

## The Festival Ends

The next big thing happened after the festival was over.

Most people head back to town on the last day.

Many even catch a flight home to their own countries in Europe that same day.

But we had no reason at all to hurry, so we decided to stay on at the festival grounds.

A lot of our friends were staying after it ended to help clean up, and we planned to join in.

During the day we helped pick up trash and so on, and got the cleanup done.

Big jobs like taking down the stage were handled by a professional crew, so there was nothing for us to do there.

Once most of the cleanup was finished and everyone could catch their breath, a staff meal was served, and we all ate together.

"Finally over," we told each other. "That was fun."

## The Main Event

But this was where things really got interesting.

Everyone had finished their work, the festival had ended in success, and we were all filled with relief and joy. Right at that moment, the after-party started.

What you'd call a wrap party.

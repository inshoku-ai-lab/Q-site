## Among Fellow Japanese

Y spoke fluent English, and I-chan's English had been getting better and better since she came to Morocco, so the two of them were enjoying their conversations with everyone.

My English had come a long way since Morocco too, but at bottom I was still someone who once scored a single point on a junior high English test.

Improve that tenfold and you're still only at about ten points, so I couldn't speak English all that freely, and it had left me with a certain frustration.

Now, though, we were all Japanese, and we could say anything and be understood.

Being able to talk freely without holding back, for the first time in a long while, made our camping trip twice as much fun.

## Full Moon

We had come out to camp partly because we wanted to enjoy nature, but also because we wanted to celebrate the full moon.

For psytrance travelers like us, the way to celebrate the full moon is with LSD.

In the most beautiful setting imaginable — desert, oasis, waterfall, lake — we wanted to take LSD, get completely high, and celebrate the full moon.

That one wish was what held us together and drove us out to camp at this waterfall.

At last month's full moon, we'd had friends we could trust and a solid hut, so we could get as high as we liked without worrying.

This time, though, it was just the three of us, out in nature with nothing around us.

We were a little on edge, but we got everything ready in advance to take the worry out of it.

## Preparation

While it was still light, we cooked so there would be food ready whenever we wanted it, and made sure we had plenty of drinking water.

We laid out warm clothes, too, and set up the beds so we could fall straight asleep if we got tired.

And so we could smoke as much cannabis as we wanted, we prepped the tobacco and the rest ahead of time and crumbled up the hash.

That was how perfectly ready we were when the full moon came.

Watching the blazing red sun go down, we took the LSD.

When we turned around, the full moon was rising behind us.

Everything was perfectly in place.

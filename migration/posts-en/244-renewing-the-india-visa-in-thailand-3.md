## The Bribe

In my pocket I had the equivalent of about six thousand yen — around fifty dollars.

By Thai prices at the time, that was several days of an average daily wage.

But the cops were greedy, and they were not about to settle for that.

They threatened to hold me unless I handed over the equivalent of thirty thousand yen — about two hundred and forty dollars.

For better or worse, the guesthouse I was staying in was only about a hundred meters (three hundred feet) away, so they let me walk back and get the money.

They kept my passport the whole time.

Until I paid, I was not going anywhere.

What I had was not cash but traveler's checks, which cannot be cashed without the owner's signature and an ID to go with it.

The cops said that was fine.

They must have known some illegal way to cash them.

I had a much bigger stash of hash hidden in my room, and if they had followed me in, this would have stopped being something a bribe could fix, so I was sweating the whole way — but the only thing they seemed to care about was the cash in front of them.

I handed over the traveler's checks, and at long last they let me go.

## Afterward

Back in my room I tried to settle down, but my knees were still shaking and there was no settling down.

Right after being fleeced for a bribe, I rolled a big joint of that hash and smoked myself calm, and that finally let me sleep.

It had been a genuinely terrible day.

The cops were bad, but the wall of cockroaches was its own kind of trauma.

The nausea and the shaking knees will stay with me.

## Daybreak

Around dawn, just when I thought I had finally gotten to sleep, the guy in the next room came home drunk.

From the talking and the noise, it was clear a middle-aged Western guy had brought a Thai prostitute back with him.

Before long, loud moaning started coming through the thin wall.

The shameless volume at daybreak made me angry, and so did the wall, which was far too thin.

And to top it all off, when they were done, they started arguing at the top of their lungs.

Their voices carried through the whole floor of the guesthouse, and the other guests, furious by now at the dawn racket, got out of bed and started protesting.

Just the worst. All of it.

In the end the Thai prostitute left the guesthouse and it went quiet, but all of it was the worst.

The next morning, I got up and moved to a different guesthouse right away.

No way I could stay in a place like that.

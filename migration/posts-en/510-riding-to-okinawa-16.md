## To Okinawa

From Sakurajima I took a ferry across to Kagoshima City, then changed to another ferry bound for Naha, Okinawa's capital.

The ferry had sleeping berths, and the plan was to spend one night on board and wake up in Naha.

Once I was aboard, there were, naturally, a lot of Okinawans, and the atmosphere was different.

Overall, the passengers had darker skin and more pronounced features.

It was my first time meeting people from Okinawa, so the difference felt fresh.

Something about them felt warmer than people on Honshu.

Just imagining the Okinawa trip ahead of me got me excited.

## Naha

By the next day, the ferry was in Okinawan waters.

When I stepped outside, the air had clearly changed.

It was already past the middle of December, but there was no winter in that air.

I had finally escaped the Honshu winter.

I had been pedaling through the cold, hurrying toward somewhere warm, and in the end I got out before the cold could catch up with me.

Having made it this far, I felt I was in the clear.

No more camping in dread of the cold, or camping glued to the nearest hot spring.

Coming to Okinawa had given me a freedom that was physical as well as mental.

## Rain

Unfortunately, it was raining when the ferry got into Naha.

It was the first rain since I'd left Osaka.

Thankfully, I was already in Okinawa, so the rain didn't make it cold.

The terminal for the Kagoshima–Naha ferry and the one for the Naha–Ishigaki ferry were some distance apart, so I had to ride between them.

It would have been silly to spend the whole day sitting at the terminal, so I decided to push through the rain to the next one.

I was really grateful the rain wasn't cold.

## To Ishigaki Island

I pedaled on and reached the terminal for the Ishigaki Island ferry.

I made it just in time, with thirty minutes to go before the ferry sailed.

I went straight aboard and headed for my berth.

I got out of my clothes and dried off from the rain.

I was soaked through, but my bike bags were waterproof, so my gear was fine.

By tomorrow I'd be on Ishigaki Island.

It was bound to be even warmer there.

I turned in, full of anticipation.

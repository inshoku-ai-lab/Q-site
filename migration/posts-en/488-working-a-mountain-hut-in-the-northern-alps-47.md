## Pouring Drinks

I went around pouring drinks for people one at a time, making conversation as I went.

A lot of it was social courtesy, but even so, facing each of those personalities one on one still drew me a step closer to them.

With people who had looked out for me, or coworkers I knew well, sharing a drink was easy, but sharing one with someone when the stress ran both ways was a real challenge.

There was a particular tension with the three guys who had been close with I-chan.

We'd gotten along well when I first worked at the big hut, but when my job at the small hut was over and I came back to the big hut, for some reason they were keeping their distance.

I had wondered whether it was tied to I-chan pulling away from me, but I found out later that all three of them apparently had a quiet crush on her, and the jealousy that came with it was aimed at me.

Since things with I-chan were going nowhere, I had done nothing to earn that jealousy, and my frustration had nowhere to go.

Quite a human drama I'd been dragged into.

## K

Working my way down the line, I finally came to K, the one I-chan had fallen for.

By then I was pretty drunk myself.

I'd never been in the habit of drinking, but working at the mountain huts had pulled me into the nightly drink with dinner.

My tolerance went up with every day I drank, but genetics still counted for something, and I got drunk fairly fast.

Already drunk, I poured K a drink.

Talking with him, I could tell he hadn't heard anything from I-chan yet.

Maybe that was her way of being considerate.

After some small talk, I turned serious and told him that whatever happened from here on, we were friends, and I was counting on him.

I said it with the future between him and I-chan in mind, but at that point K probably didn't really understand.

To him it may have sounded like the kind of heartfelt speech people make over drinks.

And honestly, I could never have gotten through a corny conversation like that without a drink in me.

## Gandalf's Wish

Communal life began without any of us really knowing what was going on.

Probably none of us had any particular goal.

Looking back now, though, Gandalf seems to have had something in mind.

Maybe he wanted to use the chance to let something like a community form on its own.

But he never told us so, and he encouraged each of us simply to be as we were.

He really didn't say a word.

He smiled on everyone, shared his knowledge and his love, and treated each of us with kindness and acceptance, and that was all.

## Broke

The friends who gathered there hadn't come because they especially wanted a communal life, either; it just looked like fun, somehow, and they liked Gandalf, so they came.

What a lot of us did have in common was that we had no money.

The only ones with a normal amount of money were Gandalf and Y.

After them came me, with about 100,000 yen in savings (around eight hundred dollars), and F, a Moroccan, whose savings were normal by Moroccan income standards.

Everyone else was spectacularly broke.

My partner I-chan was down to less than 10,000 yen, about eighty dollars, and M, a German, and C, a Frenchman, weren't much better off than she was.

A-kun, a Moroccan, O, an Australian, and R, an Englishwoman, had been completely broke from the start.

## O's Nerve

But the one who stood out from everyone here was O.

She hadn't come to the festival and ended up broke; she'd been broke before she came, broke the whole way here, and planned to keep traveling broke after this too, a true master of being broke, down to the bone.

Apparently she had been traveling freely all along without ever touching money, loved the freedom of it, and didn't mind having no money in the least.

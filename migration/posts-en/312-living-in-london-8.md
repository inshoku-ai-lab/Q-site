## Charas

There was another reason I wanted to see T: he had brought hash over from India and was selling it to his friends.

Through N, who was into reggae, I had been getting dried Jamaican cannabis and the like, but after close to a year traveling in India, having carried my chillum all the way to London, what I really wanted was Indian charas — hash resin — in that chillum.

N loved the chillum too, so smoking one together carried a deeper meaning between friends.

T had wrapped the charas in plastic wrap a gram at a time, swallowed the whole lot before boarding the plane, and smuggled it into London that way.

T was young, with little life experience, and he did not know much about how to make money.

The way to make quick money and keep traveling was that kind of risk business.

He had chosen to take the risk and live his life to the fullest.

## LSD

Hardly any of the people living in the squat house held down a real job.

Some made their money from the art they made, and some made it buying and selling drugs.

The artists' group handled party drugs — cannabis, LSD, ecstasy — while the junkies' group handled hard drugs: heroin, cocaine, ketamine, amphetamines.

LSD was the only thing that interested me; nothing else held any interest at all.

Making use of the visit, I managed to get hold of a small piece of LSD from one of the squat's residents.

The surprise was that it came cheaper than it went for in Goa, back in India.

The story was that it arrived straight from the makers in Amsterdam, and that the short distance kept the price down.

And compared with Goa, LSD was not in much demand in London — a specialty item only a few obsessives cared to take, traded cheaply inside a small circle of friends.

## Parties

I had been waiting eagerly for a chance at LSD like this.

From what I had heard, London had a lot of famous Goa trance and psytrance DJs and a lot of interesting parties, so I had been looking forward to slipping into the parties there.

And for that, I needed this LSD.

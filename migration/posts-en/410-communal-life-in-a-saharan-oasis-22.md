## The Two of Them

Things between R and C were starting to turn into something.

But before any romance could grow, it seems C had begun to dwell on how difficult R was to live with.

She was already getting on people's nerves in all sorts of ways, and she was broke with nowhere to go; he probably balked at taking her on.

For a few days the two of them had been getting closer, but little by little C started to find R tiresome.

## Cornered

With nothing going right, R had been clinging to the hope of something with C the way a drowning woman grabs at straws, and now that C was keeping his distance, she was cornered.

Communal life wasn't working out, C was pulling away, and she didn't even have a proper place to go home to.

Even if she had, she had no money to get there, and no nerve to hitchhike.

Backed into a corner, R sank deeper and deeper into her own darkness.

## Talking It Through

Everyone in the hut, each in their own way, sat down with R and tried to find some way to help, but she was in too deep for anyone to do anything.

Meanwhile, I-chan and I were having a great time as a couple, and so were O and A-kun.

M was shining, free as ever, and Y was enjoying his taste of communal life.

Everyone else was doing fine, and R alone kept sinking.

We all tried gently to smooth things over, but nothing worked at all.

All the stress R had bottled up turned straight into stress for everyone in the hut.

She had nowhere to go, and we couldn't just throw her out, but she was no help to our communal life either; all she did was eat for free and complain, and she became an extraordinarily hard person to have around.

The one who suffered most was Gandalf, who came from the same country.

As one of the few native English speakers there, Gandalf made the perfect target.

She unloaded her complaints on him day and night.

Bringing R here had been his doing, but he couldn't very well tell her to get out.

This was one worry even the old wizard couldn't do a thing about.

## F

F, a Moroccan man of forty-nine, was a gentle older guy with gentle eyes, and he was especially gentle with I-chan and me.

Maybe he felt as if we were his own kids.

And he really was about the age of our parents.

He was an old friend of Gandalf's and was there from the very beginning to the very end, but until now I haven't said much about him.

That's because there's no way to write about him properly in the free posts.

## Cannabis

He lived in Tangier and made his living selling and distributing cannabis and hash, a kindhearted older guy who served as our cannabis supplier during our communal life.

In Morocco there's a pipe for smoking cannabis, a long, thin tube about fifty centimeters (twenty inches) long.

They called it kif, so I figured kif was the word for the pipe, but when I looked it up online just now, it seems kif is what they call the cannabis itself.

A new discovery, twenty years later.

All through our communal life, we smoked cannabis out of that long pipe several times a day, every day.

Sometimes we loaded it with hashish (cannabis resin), sometimes with plain cannabis, and sometimes with a mix of the two.

This was the lifeblood of our communal life.

We all split up the work: hauling water, cooking, washing dishes, buying food.

As the reward for that work, passing the kif around the circle became part of our daily routine.

By day we smoked the kif, and at night I kneaded the mix for the chillum.

This habit of smoking cannabis was the joy we all had in common, a culture we could share.

That's simply what hippie traveler culture, and psytrance traveler culture, are.

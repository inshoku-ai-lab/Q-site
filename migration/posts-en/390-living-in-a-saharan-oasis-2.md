## Camping

So far, the group was just the two of us, I-chan and me, plus my Japanese friend Y.

Then there was Gandalf and his attendant, A-kun.

The rest of the members were supposed to arrive the next day.

It seemed Gandalf had picked them from among the people he'd taken a liking to while organizing the festival, choosing travelers who had time to spare.

It was an honor to have made the cut.

No doubt some fascinating people were on their way, but with more than ten of us living in this hut, it would be far too cramped.

As a couple, we wanted some privacy, so we chose to camp outside the hut.

I'd never imagined our tent would come in handy like this, but it was a relief to have a private space of our own.

## Meals

Inside the hut, a gas stove and cooking gear were already set up, along with some food.

Y used those supplies to cook for everyone.

During his working holiday in Canada, he'd worked at a Japanese restaurant and picked up the basics of cooking there, and as he cooked in the hut, he taught us those basics too.

We couldn't have asked for more.

Life at this oasis looked like it would teach us all kinds of things, from all kinds of people.

## The Next Day

Gandalf went into town and came back with the rest of the members.

The newcomers were F, an older Moroccan guy, 49; O, an Australian woman, 25; M, a young German guy, 23; R, an Englishwoman, 40; and C, a Frenchman, 42.

At first there was some distance between us, and we knew nothing about one another, but living together day after day, we came to know each other well.

I'd like to write about each of their personalities later on.

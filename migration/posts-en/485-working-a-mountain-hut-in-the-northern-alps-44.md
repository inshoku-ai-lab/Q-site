## Acceptance

Hearing her out, I could accept it.

Even while we were traveling, I'd often had my doubts about how independent and self-reliant she was, and more than once we'd talked about her standing on her own two feet emotionally.

Splitting up was a shame, but if we were splitting up because she had become independent and grown, then painful as it was, I could accept it.

Why she'd been so cold to me, I couldn't understand, but even so, I could make my peace with it.

Thank you for everything.

## Coming Down

The big hut was closed up for the season, and it was time to come down the mountain.

My job at the mountain huts ended, and so did my relationship with I-chan.

It hurt badly, but thinking of what was best for her, I could accept it and send her on her way.

To have built a relationship on trust in each other, and to be able to part with love, was the greatest honor I could have asked for.

I made my way down the mountain, taking in the sudden emotional shift.

## The Wrap Party

With the big hut closed, all the company's other huts were finished too, and the season's work in the mountains was over.

To close it all out, everyone gathered at a big hot-spring inn for a huge banquet: the staff from all the company's huts, the people from the office, and the people from every business we dealt with.

On the way to the banquet, I-chan stopped me again.

She said she needed to talk.

We stepped a little way off the road and started talking.

She seemed to find it hard to bring up, but then she just came out with it.

According to I-chan, she had fallen for someone.

Huh??? ....

I was speechless.

Wait, what? Didn't she break up with me because she wanted to be independent?

She'd said she wanted to stand on her own and live her own life, and that was why I could accept the breakup.

It hadn't been easy to accept, but I'd forced myself to, telling myself that if I really cared about her, accepting it was the right thing to do.

But if she'd fallen for someone, that was a completely different story.

So what had all that talk about independence been?

When two people live apart for a while, one of them may well fall for someone else.

That, I could accept.

But breaking up with me over some other reason, without telling me the one thing that actually mattered: what was that?

I had never been so confused.

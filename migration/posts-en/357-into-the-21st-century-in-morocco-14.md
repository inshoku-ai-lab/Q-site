## The Festival Site

We had plenty of time, so we decided to go and take a look at the place where the festival was supposed to happen.

About twenty minutes out of town in a shared taxi, we got off the bus where the driver told us to.

This was apparently where the festival was going to be held.

At least, this seemed to be the place the festival flyer was talking about.

Along the road there were about five houses and a few palm trees, and nothing else.

What you noticed was desert, hard ground stretching out in every direction.

When people think of the Sahara, I imagine most of them picture dunes of fine, drifting sand.

So did we, but here the desert was hard ground and stones.

The earth wasn't ocher so much as a kind of reddish brown.

Far off, we could see a range of dark brown mountains.

## The Oasis

We decided to walk in the direction the driver had pointed.

After about ten minutes across empty wasteland, a shining surface of water came into view.

We had been walking in search of an oasis, but when water actually appears where there's nothing to see but desert, it makes you pretty happy.

We picked up our pace and headed for the water's edge.

## Something Moving

As we got near the water, something moving caught my eye.

Closer still, the moving thing we'd seen in the distance turned out to be, of all things, a person.

Closer still, we could make out that the person was very big, wore a straw hat, and had on a djellaba, the traditional Moroccan dress, a kind of robe.

You know the robe: the kind wizards wear.

Closer still, we could see that he had a long white beard and long white hair.

The glasses over his eyes were round, like John Lennon's.

He looked exactly like the pictures that come up when you do an image search for "old wizard."

Many years later, I found out that a character in the Lord of the Rings movies was him exactly: Gandalf.

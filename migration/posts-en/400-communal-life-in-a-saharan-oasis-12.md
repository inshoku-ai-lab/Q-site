## M's Flow

M, a young German, was going through a time of change.

Something had been on his mind, and he'd left Germany and come to Morocco.

The usual traveler is someone like Y, who saves up, gets all the gear in order, and sets out only once everything is ready.

M was the complete opposite: he'd simply taken off on impulse and was trying to live by going with the flow.

It was a way of life that seemed to rebel against the German trait of careful planning.

In his case, it didn't look like sloppy, aimless drifting so much as a deliberate test of the limits of his own life.

## Traveling with No Money

His way of testing those limits was to keep traveling even after the money ran out.

In a way, he was a beginner's version of O.

The life skill he'd taught himself was to take things lightly.

He was trying to plant himself at the exact opposite end from the German who thinks everything through far too heavily.

At the festival he'd worked as a volunteer, just like O, and once it was over, Gandalf invited him to the oasis to join the communal life.

He had caught an interesting flow, and he was riding it.

## Blossoming

This communal life was where M's ideals, and his flow, had begun to blossom.

Meeting O, a mentor in the art of wandering, must have had a lot to do with it, and you could also say it was Gandalf's big heart that let him bloom.

M had started out with a fairly reserved, German, engineer-type personality, but he was loosening up more and more, and his own bright side was picking up speed fast.

That momentum showed in the way he talked, too: his English, which had already been decent, was quickly turning into something he spoke as if it were his native language.

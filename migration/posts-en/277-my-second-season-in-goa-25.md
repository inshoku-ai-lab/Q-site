## Speedball

That habit of pushing everything to the limit showed in how they played with drugs, too: most travelers touched nothing beyond cannabis and the hallucinogens people call psychedelics, LSD and MDMA and the like, while the Crew went in for the powders as well — cocaine and heroin.

The momentum carried them further still, all the way to the monster known as the speedball.

The idea is that you mix cocaine and heroin, take them together, and get the ultimate rush and the ultimate ease at the same moment — a high in unknown territory.

I have never done it, so I cannot say, but from what people told me, the pleasure is as good as pleasure gets.

And the price of that pleasure is not just your health but your life.

## A Snapped Wire

Then one day V, one of the ○○ Crew, who had been messing around with speedballs, crossed the line.

He blacked out while he was still high.

The others panicked and called an ambulance, and because the ambulance got to him, he came through with his life, barely.

He came back from the hospital, and at a glance the treatment looked like a success, as though nothing had happened at all — but from that point on V began saying and doing strange things.

Apparently one of the wires in his head had snapped.

They had all been outside the frame of common sense to begin with, but V's values shifted to somewhere far outside even the frame they had stepped outside of.

Nothing put the brakes on anything anymore, and he started driving hard toward extreme ideas.

One time he ignored everyone trying to stop him, took a massive dose of LSD, and went around telling people "I have become God," which earned him nothing but cold looks.

I can understand it now — I think what he was going through was an experience of oneness.

But no one around him understood, and he was treated as a weirdo.

A wire in his head really had snapped, so it is hard to blame them.

V went on repeating the same extreme, strange behavior, until in the end a friend contacted his family and they came all the way to Goa to collect him.

Goa's freedom was what brought people their finest experiences and their growth, and that same freedom was a double-edged sword: half a step off and you dropped to the bottom of the abyss.

## Drama

First of all, the conversation had to keep going, or we'd get nowhere.

I asked I-chan who it was she'd fallen for.

But she kept dodging the question and wouldn't tell me.

So I went through the likely candidates, one man at a time.

It turned out to be K, five years younger than me, whom I introduced in [this post](/posts/working-a-mountain-hut-in-the-northern-alps-9-vagabond-chronicles-450).

## Feelings

I had been close with K too, so my feelings were mixed.

Strangely, though, I felt no jealousy at all.

Toward K I felt nothing but friendship and warmth.

What I felt was disappointment about the relationship between I-chan and me.

We had spent two years traveling together, through the good times and the hard ones, and I couldn't get over how easily it had all ended.

There had been every chance to talk it through, and if she had just been honest with me, I would have been big enough to accept it.

Instead, she had broken up with me for some other reason, and only at the very last moment told me the truth.

## Confession

Apparently she was about to go and tell him how she felt.

By now, the only thing on I-chan's mind was whether things would work out with him.

Even telling me the truth felt less like doing right by me and more like clearing the way for her confession to go well.

Looking back later, I understood why she had been so strangely cold to me: she didn't want K to see the two of us being close.

Our relationship had ended months before.

All I-chan could think about was how to get close to K and make things work with him.

And to that end, she seemed to have been consumed by one question: how to end things with me, since I was in the way.

## The Auction

I've already told the story of the leather jacket I was given while working part-time in London, in [this earlier post](/posts/living-in-london-37-vagabond-chronicles-341).

It looked like a very expensive jacket, so I had shipped it to Japan, planning to sell it at auction there.

Looking into it further, I found out that leather jackets from this brand, Chrome Hearts, were worth quite a lot, so I decided to try selling it at auction for real.

From what I read, auctions that end on a Saturday night tend to draw higher prices, so I set mine to close then.

To catch people's eye, I started the bidding at one yen and added every option there was for making a listing stand out.

On top of that, I made the rounds of every message board on the internet where Chrome Hearts fans gathered, spreading the word that I had an auction going that started at one yen.

Honestly, I couldn't quite bring myself to believe it would really sell for much, but letting a chance that had come my way slip right past me didn't sit well with me as a traveler.

I wanted to do everything I could.

## The Sale

For a while after the auction opened, nobody bid at all, but the number of people adding it to their watch lists kept growing.

About three days before the end, bids slowly started coming in.

Two days out, the price went up further, and on the last day things heated up even more.

In the end, the leather jacket I had been given for free sold for a full 360,000 yen, close to three thousand dollars.

A windfall I never in a million years saw coming.

I had always traveled on the cheap, trip after trip, so I almost never had as much as 360,000 yen in cash in my hands.

My last trip had started out on 400,000 yen, a little over three thousand dollars.

## Splitting It

I decided to split the windfall with I-chan.

She was the partner who had shared the good times and the hard times with me in London, so I felt that money from London ought to be shared with her.

We decided to use it to buy the gear we would need for our jobs at the mountain hut.

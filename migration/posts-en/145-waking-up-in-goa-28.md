## A Break

The day Goa Gil's Christmas party ended, I ate something and went to sleep.

My brain and body had been driven all the way to their limit, and a single night's sleep wasn't enough to bring them back — it took about twenty-four hours of sleeping, with one light meal in the middle, before I recovered.

The day I was back on my feet, I headed for the restaurant we always went to and traded Goa Gil stories with my friends.

We were in complete agreement, and it came down to one line: Goa Gil was unbelievable.

There was something divine about the experience, and a single party was all it took to convert us into believers in Goa trance.

Not that we had a religion to begin with, but there was some enormous power in Goa trance, and I wanted to go deeper into that world.

Everyone had felt something like a mystical experience at the Goa Gil party, but we had no words for what had moved us.

Talking about a feeling like that felt embarrassing.

But the feeling had hold of us and wouldn't let go, radiating an overwhelming pull, shining deep down inside.

## Seasoned Travelers

There were a few encounters from around this time that stayed with me.

One was sitting down for tea after a party on the beach with a group of older Western guys — men with real presence, who had been coming to Goa for years.

In his broken English, Y asked if they came to Goa often.

The first said this was his eighth year. The next one said, "Twelve years for me." And the third said twenty.

Every one of them had come back every year since the first, without missing one.

They were seasoned travelers, and the way they told it, they had wandered the world before landing in Goa, where they met Goa trance and woke up.

From then on, it seems, Goa sat at the center of their lives, and they came back every year.

When we said this was our first time in Goa and that we were stunned by how incredible it was, they said, welcome to Goa, and took us into the circle.

To us they were the ones ahead of us in travel, in life, and in Goa, and it made me happy to be accepted by travelers that seasoned.

## The Road Called Goa Trance

Another was a Japanese friend who had moved into the neighborhood.

It was his first time in Goa, but back in Japan he had been going to trance parties with friends who had been to Goa over and over.

He was deep in that culture, and for him a trance party was something worth staking your life on.

When I told him my age, he was startled at how young I was, and in the same breath he told me it was a good thing I had found my purpose in life this young.

At the time I had no idea what he was talking about, but after the Christmas Goa Gil party I could more or less understand what he had meant.

I had already been through it, and I had already taken a step onto a road with no way back.

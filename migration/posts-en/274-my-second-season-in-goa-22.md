## An Eccentric, Amplified

N's strangeness was gaining momentum, fed by the free land that was Goa, by the Goa trance parties, and by the consciousness-amplifying effect of large amounts of LSD.

At a Goa trance party there is no set way to dance, the way there is in tango or salsa, ballet or aerobics; everyone dances freely, however they want, with everything they have.

Everyone is thinking about dancing in whatever way feels good to them, concentrating on syncing up with the sound.

Some do think about how cool they look, or how beautiful, how cute, but most people do not care much about any of that.

And at a Goa trance party, not caring about it is precisely what makes you cool.

But N, eccentric to the core, was concentrating on how bad, how lame he could make his dancing.

And not for five or ten minutes either — he kept it up all night, eight hours straight, in single-minded pursuit of looking as uncool as possible.

The way he saw it, destroying the values he had built up until then was the thing that would lead to his own liberation, and dancing lame was how he was trying to break through that line.

## The LSD Notebook

This was N's first time in India, and his first time in Goa.

It was also his first time sinking all the way into Goa's LSD party culture, and the sheer freedom of it hit him hard, leaving him dazed, in something like culture shock.

Dazed as he was, his curiosity was wide open, and he was trying to absorb everything from every moment and every event.

To accelerate that momentum, he started keeping something he called an LSD notebook.

It was written under one strict rule: entries could only go in while he was high on LSD.

All kinds of insights come to you while you are gone on LSD, but in that altered state of consciousness you often forget them.

He had started writing to keep that from happening.

The contents, though, were hard to follow for anyone who had not been through the same state of consciousness — rambling, like being told about a dream someone had the night before.

To him, it was a magic notebook, one where he had written down secrets of tremendous importance, packed with the greatest wisdom of the fourth-dimensional world.

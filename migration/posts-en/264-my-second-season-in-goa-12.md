## The New Year's Party

Paraiso, the party's proper venue, had been shut down by the police, so this year's party was moved to a newly built place called Hilltop.

The rumor was that Hilltop's owner had ties to the senior police officers, so parties there would never be shut down.

In exchange, the venue was ringed with a high wall, and there was no breaking in.

And it charged a steep entry fee to raise the bribe money for those officers.

For a Goa person it was an unforgivable thing to do, but since this was the only party there was, everyone grudgingly paid and went in.

Now, as someone who knows a fair amount about the underside of the world, I suspect Paraiso was shut down as part of a conspiracy between the senior police and Hilltop's owner.

## Trespassing

As a Goa person, I could not accept a party walled in and charging an entry fee.

A party should be free, I believed, something the willing shared among themselves.

So, boldly, I attempted to trespass.

Paying could wait until after I had taken my shot at trespassing.

We worked our way around to the very back of the venue, found a stretch where the wall dropped low, and pulled off a magnificent trespass.

It was a self-serving theory, and we were trespassing without paying, but we were satisfied that we were doing the right thing.

## The Party

Inside, giant speakers stood in a row and the place was already going strong.

Of all the parties I had been to, this was the biggest, with the finest speakers.

They had gone to the trouble of importing them from Europe, apparently.

We headed for the chai stall, and over a chai we got a chillum ready to smoke.

Among the Japanese who came out to Goa, getting a chillum ready was called kneading the chillum.

Mixing the charas and tobacco carefully together looks like kneading, and that is where the phrase came from.

With the top-grade cream — hash resin — I had carried down from the Himalayas, I passed my proud new chillum around the circle.

The smoke raced down my throat, so overwhelmingly clean that it made me wonder what I had been smoking all this time, and I was high in an instant, my mind going calm and easy in the same moment.

Bringing in the New Year with the finest cream and the finest chillum made me proud.

With everything ready, I took the LSD and headed for the dance floor.

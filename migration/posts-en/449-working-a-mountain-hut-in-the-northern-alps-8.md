## R's True Colors

R, the quiet twenty-year-old with the hard stare, was gradually getting used to the work.

As he settled in, his character changed dramatically.

He wasn't really the quiet type after all; he was apparently just shy with strangers to an extreme degree.

On top of that, he'd apparently been a notorious delinquent back home in Tochigi, strutting through the streets in a silver suit.

According to him, you'd have a hard time finding a rougher place than Tochigi.

His hard stare, it seemed, wasn't only shyness; his history had something to do with it too.

And there was more: he had eyeshadow tattooed along the rims of his eyes.

That eyeshadow was part of what made his stare so hard.

Tattooing the rims of your eyes is a frightening thing that would hardly occur to most people, but I suppose it was one more way of showing off in the delinquent world.

## Background

As R slowly got used to people, his bizarre history became the liveliest topic of conversation.

He apparently had seven siblings, and so he'd lived in extreme poverty as a child.

That was what had led to his delinquent years.

A close look at his hands showed they were covered in *konjo-yaki*.

*Konjo-yaki* is when delinquent kids press a lit cigarette into the backs of their own hands to brand themselves, as proof of their guts.

The skin on the backs of his hands looked like it had melted from the burns and then hardened again, a strange, lava-like skin.

He never took his shirt off, maybe because of some fierce tattoos on his back and chest, and he always kept his arms wrapped in bandages.

Someone like him naturally ended up with a nickname to match, but saying it would give away who he is, so I'll keep it to myself.

## Twenty

R was only twenty, but underneath, he was nothing like a twenty-year-old.

He was shy, but he had nerve, and he was always full of absolute confidence.

It was the kind of absolute confidence that seemed to come from having made it through some real bloodbaths, and later on he would boast that he could win a fight against anyone within his line of sight.

R was fun to watch from the sidelines, but he was such an extreme delinquent that I never quite managed to become close friends with him.

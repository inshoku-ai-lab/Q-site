## The Escape

At the police station each of us was made to sign a form.

Name and passport number written on a sheet of paper, and that was it.

That should have been the end of it, but we were kept there waiting for nothing.

At some point the Israeli couple slipped away too, and in the end the only ones left were the Japanese.

Whether the Indian police had even noticed was not clear.

You reading this may be thinking, "There's no way they didn't notice!" — but India is that kind of country.

A country where a thing like that can actually work.

However long we waited, nothing moved, and everyone drifted around the station grounds.

I drifted over toward the front entrance and looked around.

Not one person seemed to be paying me any attention.

I took half a step out and glanced back, and still nobody cared.

Another half step, and another, and somewhere in there I was outside the station grounds, and since nobody cared, I decided to simply walk back to the guesthouse.

## The Guesthouse

I caught a rickshaw and had it take me up to Vashisht.

My heart was thumping a little, but in the end nothing came of it.

I was embarrassed by how earnest and how clueless we Japanese were, but I also felt a certain pride at having gotten myself out of it.

Back in the room my roommate K, rubbing the sleep out of his eyes, gave me a warm welcome home.

K had once taken so much LSD that he nearly lost his mind, and after a promise to his girlfriend he had quit LSD for good.

He didn't come to the parties, of course, but having been through it himself he understood a good deal.

I explained that things had gotten complicated and that I had run off, and told him I was heading to the hot springs so as not to disturb him just after he had woken up.

When all was said and done, the hot springs in Vashisht were unbeatable, and washing off the sweat after a party was the greatest pleasure there was.

When I got back to the guesthouse, Y, who had gone missing, was waiting for me.

Hearing how things had turned out for him surprised me even more.

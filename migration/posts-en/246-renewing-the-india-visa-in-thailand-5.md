## Beach Life

When we reached Koh Chang, we took a bungalow along the beach.

We weren't planning to stay all that long, so we just found a place that felt right and settled in.

We enjoyed the beach, enjoyed the nature, enjoyed the sunsets, and within a few days we were bored.

It was a resort kind of place, the sort couples with money come to for a short vacation, so it was stylish enough, but prices ran on the high side and for a backpacker there was nothing interesting about it at all.

The other travelers there felt more like tourists, and were no fun at all.

I kept thinking back to the exciting encounters I'd had in India, and it left me feeling hollow.

Even so, I was glad to be spending time with my closest friends.

## Withdrawal

R's withdrawal, after six months of taking opium with no restraint at all, kept getting worse, and he stayed crouched in a corner of the bungalow, his face hollowed out.

By his own account, withdrawal was burning hot and freezing cold, his whole body splitting apart with pain, nausea so bad he could not even sleep — he went on and on, working through every vague complaint a person could imagine.

On top of that, a greasy sweat seeped out of him from head to foot, and a strange body odor hung in the air around him.

To put it plainly, it felt like looking at the very worst part of what a human being is.

Watching that up close, I was certain I would never take an interest in opium, and never get hooked.

## The Year 2000

After a few days on Koh Chang, comfortable and boring in equal measure, we went back to Bangkok.

Khao San Road was overflowing with travelers from every part of the world.

All of this is November 1999, just before the year turned over into 2000.

The talk among the travelers was all about the New Year's psytrance parties celebrating the millennium.

Some were going to Bali to celebrate the year 2000, others to Koh Phangan, Australia, Goa, South Africa; the travelers were all making their preparations for the end of the year.

And at that point, travelers like that were gathered on Khao San Road in Bangkok.

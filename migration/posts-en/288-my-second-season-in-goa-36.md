## Unknown Territory

With the wall in the unconscious between U and me removed, my consciousness was released into unknown territory.

Freed from the framework of space and time, it pushed its exploration on into a new dimension.

I wasn't the only one wrecked on LSD — every friend there with me was further gone than they had ever been.

In the middle of that, our conversation moved on to how this world is put together out of energy.

I was too far gone to remember now exactly what we said, but it was the kind of conversation where grasping the framework of the world lets you see the world whole, from above.

But the most astonishing part of the experience wasn't what we talked about. It was the way the conversation came together.

## T

T was there too, a close friend from back in Vashisht, where we had spent all our time cutting loose together.

She had been born into a relatively well-off family, harmonious and wanting for nothing, and from early childhood she was far and away the smartest — good taste, good looks, popular with everyone, class president straight through elementary school.

She was a live-action Shizuka-chan, the ideal girl from the Doraemon cartoons, and in that classroom she was, overwhelmingly, one of the winners.

That unbroken experience of having everything, meeting her intelligence and that sharp sense of hers, brought her at twelve to an enlightenment of her own, and to renouncing worldly desire.

From then on, still an elementary school kid, she lost interest in the outer world and became a seeker exploring the inner one, and once she was grown she traveled the world.

Meeting LSD multiplied the depth of that character of hers many times over, and in the conversation at that party about how the world is put together, it came fully into its own.

Up to that point, T had rarely spoken openly about the world of consciousness she carried inside her.

Probably she had decided we wouldn't understand.

But at this party, with every last one of us accessing deep regions of consciousness, T's depth was set free.

## Oneness

By this point my consciousness and T's were completely in sync — whatever I wanted to hear about, T would start talking about.

In response I would put exactly the right question, and T would come back with the perfect answer.

In the end I knew what T's answer would be before I asked.

Is what I'm saying getting across?

What I mean is that by the time the question surfaced in my head and I was putting it to T, I already knew how T was going to answer.

Question and answer had merged into one, and my consciousness and T's had merged into one.

You could say I was making T give the answers, or that T was making me ask the questions.

It was a strange sync, in a superconscious state.

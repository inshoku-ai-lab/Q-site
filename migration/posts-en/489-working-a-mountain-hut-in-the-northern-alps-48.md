## How They Drank

The mountain men and mountain women who had worked in the huts for years could really put it away.

Up on the peaks, where the air is thin, alcohol goes to your head fast.

For people who had a drink every night up there, alcohol down in the thick air of the lowlands was like water.

And these were burly mountain people, bursting with stamina and full of fire, throwing their last party of the season.

It got pretty wild.

Naturally, nobody flew into a rage at this last party, but all around the room there were people overcome by everything they were feeling, wailing and sobbing, and others who couldn't stop laughing.

## Dead Drunk

As I went around pouring drinks and drinking with one person after another, the alcohol slowly caught up with me.

At first I was drinking to be sociable, but underneath it all, the painful feelings about things with I-chan were churning.

Eventually it turned into drowning my sorrows, and I kept drinking hard.

It was the kind of mood where I couldn't get through it without a drink.

Everyone else was beaming, the job safely done and a pile of cash in hand, while I alone sat there miserable, swallowed up by my own drinking.

It was all far too private for the people involved, so I didn't give anyone the details of what had happened with I-chan, but even without my saying a word, everyone more or less understood.

They didn't know what was hurting me, but they felt for the hurt.

With my feelings still in a tangle, I passed out drunk and fell asleep right there in the banquet hall.

Luckily, I wasn't the only one who had passed out, and the hotel itself was set up for guests who drank until they dropped, so it wasn't much of a problem.

## The Party Goes On

The mountain men and women partied hard, and it carried on into the next day.

Between drinks, they soaked in the hot springs, ate lavish meals, and then started drinking all over again.

That day, I drank in moderation and kept quiet.

In the end, nothing with I-chan was ever cleared up, and it all came to an end with my grievances still bottled up inside me.

The next morning, I headed back to Osaka, weighed down by a gloom I couldn't shake.

I said goodbye to I-chan too, but things between us were still awkward, and it turned into a cold parting.

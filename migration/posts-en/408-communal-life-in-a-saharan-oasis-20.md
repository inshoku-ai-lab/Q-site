## Romance

Of the ten of us living together, everyone except I-chan and me was single.

Gandalf, for one, had only just gotten divorced.

I suspect each of them was, subconsciously, hoping for some romance.

The ten of us were seven men and three women, an overwhelming majority of men.

The odds favored the women.

In that setting, the first romance to bloom was O's.

## O's Partner

Normally you'd figure O would pair up with Y or M, guys she might go on traveling with afterward.

But to our surprise, she became involved with A-kun, the youngest of us and a Moroccan.

To begin with, the two of them didn't even share a language.

Most people wouldn't think of getting close to someone from a different culture, a different upbringing, and a different language.

But I think O was free even of notions like that.

Maybe something clicked between them on an instinctive level, and the two of them grew close fast.

I-chan and I slept in the tent we'd pitched outside the hut, so I didn't know much about it, but I heard later that they'd been rustling and fumbling around in the middle of the night, and the others couldn't get much sleep.

How did Gandalf, who was in charge of looking after A-kun, feel about all that?

Having grown up in Morocco, a Muslim country, A-kun was probably having his first romance, and his first sexual experience.

Even for someone raised by Moroccan hippie parents, a free-love relationship with O must have come as a shock.

There was no telling where it would lead, but nothing was going to stop those two young people.

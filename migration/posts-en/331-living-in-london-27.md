## D's Decision

D was certain by now that this party was not the one we had been aiming for, and at the same time he understood perfectly well that it was already too late.

So, on the premise that everyone else believed this was the party we had been aiming for and was having a good time, he decided to let all of it slide and enjoy it to the last.

That takes a serious amount of letting go.

Nothing on this trip of his had gone right, not one thing, and he had made up his mind to let all of it go and have a good time.

As it turned out, he was having the time of his life.

This, you could say, was where a grown-up traveler showed what he was really made of.

## The Party Ends

In the end, still feeling that something was off, we spent the whole of it having a good time, and then the party was over.

The music stopped, and everyone gathered in the middle of the floor.

Now that it was finally quiet, we checked our doubts against each other's.

We looked around at each other's faces — *so this isn't DJ Tsuyoshi's event after all, is it.*

There hadn't been a single Japanese DJ in the first place.

Every one of us had felt, in some vague way, that this wasn't it, and every one of us, in our own way, had enjoyed it to the fullest right to the end.

What even is that, you might well ask — but we had had a great time, and we were oddly satisfied.

## Daybreak

We stepped outside to find the night giving way to morning, the sky going pale.

The paling sky was covered by the unnaturally low clouds that are particular to London.

Hanging leaden and thick at a level so low it felt like you could reach up and touch them, the clouds caught the morning sun and slowly turned red.

It was a stunning sight, truly stunning.

The mackerel sky lying low over us changed color in order, from one edge across, forming a magnificent rainbow spectacle.

Just getting to see that morning glow made it the best moment of all, the kind that washes everything away and leaves you wrapped in love and friendship.

Whether it had all been good or bad I couldn't tell, but there was something there that made you accept it: "this is fine as it is."

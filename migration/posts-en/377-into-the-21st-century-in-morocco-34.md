## Morning in the Desert

With hot sun pouring down on the desert, there was no staying asleep in the tent.

Outside, I could take in the whole island we were on and the lake around it.

We were right in the middle of a view that was, quite simply, beautiful.

And out of that beautiful scenery came a furious, ear-splitting shout.

A couple traveling by truck were apparently having a huge fight first thing in the morning.

The chill-out area where we were camped sat apart from the main camping area, and basically the only people camping there were people involved with the festival or people who'd broken the festival's camping rules.

So the whole place was full of travelers with very strong personalities.

Even so, the couple filling the morning with their shouting had personalities that went way beyond strong.

## A Wild Man

I had met the man a few days earlier, at the party at the hotel.

He had apparently traveled the desert on horseback several times before, and he said desert life suited him.

That didn't seem entirely untrue; he looked insanely wild.

To put it simply, he looked like a villain out of Fist of the North Star, the manga.

His dreadlocks and the huge piercings all over his face made sure you noticed him.

They weren't the neat kind of dreads a hair salon turns out, but the kind that had matted together on their own over a life in the desert.

Any way you looked at him, a personality was bursting out of him that I couldn't imagine ever fitting into society.

How he paid for his travels was a mystery, but there was a gravity to him that made you think maybe he didn't need money at all.

## An Even Wilder Woman

The man was insanely wild, but the woman topped him.

The one doing the shouting wasn't the wild man with the villain's face but the woman, who was wilder still.

Big for a woman, she had fierce-looking dreadlocks hanging down to her waist.

Her hair was red, so the sheer flashiness of her was on another level.

All kinds of accessories were tied into her hair, and a star-shaped ornament dangled over her forehead, announcing her powerful personality.

She was clearly wilder than any woman I had ever seen, and the only person I could compare her to was Janis Joplin.

But her personality and presence seemed to go beyond even Janis Joplin's.

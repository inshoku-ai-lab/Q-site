## Setting Up for a Long Stay

Delighted with the perfect house we had found, we decided to buy all sorts of household goods to make the next few months better.

The place had almost nothing in it, so we had to get it all together ourselves.

That inconvenience was probably why no one else had rented it, and that was exactly what made it work for us, since we were there for a long stay.

Mapusa was a big town about thirty minutes away by motorcycle, and there you could get just about everything you needed to live.

We bought quilts, and straw mats, and buckets and washtubs and the like.

We bought a kerosene stove to cook on, and pots, and a cutting board and a knife.

We went to a shop that sold imported food and stocked up on soy sauce and the rest, and with that we were ready for the good days ahead.

## Christmas

While all this was going on, Christmas was getting close.

Goa is a Christian state, and Christmas there is a huge event — an important day of celebration for the travelers too.

It was an important event for Y's documentary as well, but Y showed no sign of getting started.

Everything was in place, and yet the inspiration would not come, he said.

This isn't what I'm looking for, he said.

I understood that feeling well.

Goa this year, unlike the year before, was not filled with that crazy energy.

One reason was that the seriously crazy psytrance travelers, the ones who had gone all the way over the line, had all gone to South Africa for the millennium party there, so none of them were in Goa.

Others had gone to a party at Ayers Rock in Australia, and others to parties in Bali; the travelers' energy had scattered, and that was the main reason.

And there was another reason: Goa the year before had been too crazy, had drawn far too much public attention, and the Goa police had started clamping down.

That year there was a club called Paraiso, the place that had put on the legendary party where the famous DJ Goa Gil played thirty-six hours straight.

This year it was supposed to reopen, renovated at even greater expense and bigger than ever, but it had drawn too much notice and the police put a stop to it.

## The Lake

Off in the distance, we could see a lake.

I'd have loved a swim under the full moon, but the lake was the town's drinking water, and Gandalf had forbidden us to swim in it.

Lit by the full moon, the lake floated up out of the darkness, shining and regal.

We talked as we watched it glow.

About how we used to live, where we'd travel next, our dreams for the future, and life.

I-chan would go back to Japan and throw herself into theater.

Y was heading north into Europe after this, working his way back to Japan country by country.

As for me, I'd been torn between traveling on through Africa and going back to London, but I'd made up my mind: London.

## Traveler, Musician

Inside me were two selves, one that saw itself as a traveler and one that saw itself as a musician, and my mind was split between them.

I'd done a fair amount of traveling by now, and I wanted to focus on music.

That had been the original plan: live in London and concentrate on making music.

Once this camp was over, I'd head north a little at a time, traveling as I went, and make my way back to London.

I had no place to live and no job, but I'd manage.

## A Full Moon Ceremony

Sharing the full moon like this wasn't just a simple pleasure for us. It was a farewell ritual, too, and a send-off into the next stage of our lives.

I-chan and I would part ways when our travels in Morocco were over.

We weren't planning to break up as a couple, but we had no plans to see each other again, either.

We were far too young to pin down the future.

That was exactly why we needed a ceremony like this: to digest what we felt, and then distill it.

By the time the sun came up and the full moon faded into its light, whatever had been knotted up inside me had gone still, like the surface of the lake.

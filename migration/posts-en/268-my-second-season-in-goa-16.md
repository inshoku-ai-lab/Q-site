## A-san Spinning His Wheels

A-san's efforts were pointed off in entirely the wrong direction, and they kept accelerating, twisting harder as they went.

Wanting to raise his experience level in Goa, A-san started using DMT, an extremely powerful hallucinogen.

DMT is an extract of ayahuasca, the powerful healing herb used by the shamans of the Amazon in South America, and in the right setting, used properly, it can bring powerful healing, awakening, and spiritual benefit.

But someone without much experience of that kind, using it alone with no shaman to guide him, can sometimes end up mired in a demonic realm.

That was exactly what happened to A-san.

With him, the root of it was an unpleasant feeling he carried, and trying to turn that over through the DMT experience was a fundamentally twisted motive, so however far he pushed on, the twist only grew.

## A-san's Loneliness

The further A-san pushed, the more outlandish his look became.

In Goa, travelers with years behind them and real drive often chased their own individuality into a look all their own.

A-san was copying only the outlandish part of that, so he just came off as a strange guy, and he stuck out badly from everyone around him.

With that on top of everything else, people drifted away from A-san, and he was not making any new friends.

Unable to fully accept that, A-san got past it by looking down on the people who had come to Goa later than he had.

But Y saw this and said, "A-san only got to Goa this year himself, and he's making fun of people who came a few weeks later," and found A-san lamer still, and pulled further away.

Y understood that A-san had started twisting because he himself could not get inspired about the shoot, and it was exactly because he felt responsible for A-san that he wanted to avoid him all the more — a downward spiral he had fallen into.

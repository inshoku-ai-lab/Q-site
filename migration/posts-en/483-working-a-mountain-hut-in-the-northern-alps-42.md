## The Big Hut

I spent a few days at the dorm, enjoying the easy company of staff from the other huts and letting my body recover.

After that came about a month of work at the big hut where I'd been before, and then my mountain hut job would be over.

For me, the top priority was seeing my girlfriend, I-chan, again.

Just as I had grown a lot while we went our separate ways, she must have grown a lot in that time too.

I climbed the mountain full of anticipation.

The climb on my first day on the job had been pretty grueling, but after four and a half months of working in the mountains, I went up without any trouble at all.

## Reunion

When I got to the big hut, I made the rounds greeting the coworkers I'd worked with before.

I-chan was among them, and we hugged hello.

But I had a vague sense that we weren't quite connecting.

Well, we hadn't seen each other in a few months, so maybe that was only natural, I told myself, and I let it go.

Later I went to see her on a break and we talked, and sure enough, something about her was distant.

The feeling of a couple in love was completely gone.

## Distance

With how busy a mountain hut gets, finding time on a break to spend together is not easy.

A few days passed after I started working at the big hut again, but time with I-chan was hard to come by.

When we passed each other at work, she was distant and wouldn't really look me in the eye.

I wanted to ask what on earth was going on inside her, but we couldn't even have a proper conversation.

More and more, whenever I tried to get closer, she pulled away.

On top of that, the friends my age I'd been close to before I went to work at the small hut had, for some reason, started keeping their distance from me too.

I-chan spent her time happily with those friends, while I felt left out, like a kid who'd just transferred to a new school.

Even so, the rest of the staff were kind to me, and the workdays were fun. Things with I-chan, though, stayed cold.

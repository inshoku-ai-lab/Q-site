## Hashish

The block of hashish we'd bought was beautiful, and it gave off a sweet, heady smell.

To anyone who isn't interested it's just a drug, but to a cannabis lover the pollen of the cannabis plant is a magic powder, and hashish, pressed together from it, was something like a magic potion.

We went straight back to our room, packed it into the chillum I'd brought from India, and pulled the hash deep into our lungs.

For me this was something like an important ritual: smoking a good chillum of good cannabis was one of those habits in life you must never skip.

On this point I was fiercely attached, and I took pride in keeping the habit up without fail.

It was the kind of thinking you find among certain Hindu ascetics, or in the Goa hippie cult, antisocial in its way: a world with room enough to smoke cannabis and stay high was the wonderful one, and you should keep as far away as possible from anything like office work, the things you can't do stoned.

I was deep into that way of thinking back then, so I was grateful to be sitting there like this, chillum smoked, head not working, doing nothing but smiling.

## The Line

Two hundred grams (about seven ounces) of hashish is too much for just the two of us to smoke.

The idea behind buying it was to smoke half ourselves and sell the other half at twice the price, which would cover what we spent on our own share.

I could have done what I'd done in India, putting our travel money into a big load, carrying it with us, and selling it off to keep us traveling, but I didn't want to get deeply involved in that way of life, so I didn't.

That didn't mean I wasn't going to smoke, though, and if I was smoking anyway, I wanted the very best, bought cheap and in quantity.

If I was going to take on the risk of carrying cannabis anyway, I might as well take on a little more of it and save on what we smoked ourselves.

That was a line I could live with.

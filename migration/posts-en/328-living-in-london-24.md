## Slow Mood

All of us were dancing and having a good time.

Pleasant dance music was playing.

But we had come to a psytrance event, and no psytrance was playing.

The crowd felt different too.

Well, it was still early, and sooner or later the DJ would change and the psytrance would start.

Without thinking about it too hard, we waited for the next DJ.

## The Next DJ

About two hours into the dancing, the DJ changed.

Good, I thought, now the psytrance starts for real — and once again what came on was techno, slower than psytrance.

Still, the music was good, and the floor was picking up.

But the mood of the place just wasn't that of a psytrance party.

Something felt off, but the floor was building, and it carried us along and we kept enjoying ourselves.

## The Trip

In fact, right after we arrived at the party, the four of us had taken a moderate dose of LSD.

On top of that we had been smoking chillums hard, and we were wrecked out of our minds, thoroughly gone.

Out of our heads, we kept right on dancing.

Each of us turned to face our own inner world, and each of us set off on a journey of our own.

Now and then we would meet on the floor and dance together, grinning at each other, but with the LSD hitting full force and the music at deafening volume, we couldn't hold anything like a real conversation.

We'd talk a little, confirm that the other one was flying nicely and dancing, and then each go back into the music.

Every one of us wondered why no psytrance was playing, but we were enjoying ourselves plenty, so we kept dancing without thinking about it too hard.

## Gravitas

I-chan and I had both taken LSD many times in India, so we'd seen dramas like A-kun's more than once.

We were more or less used to it, but still, when you come face to face with a friend wandering around stark naked, you scramble to do something about it.

Then I looked at how calm Gandalf, D, and E were, and it made me wonder whether there was any need to panic.

As Gandalf saw it, apparently, he wanted to do something but couldn't, and he wasn't in any state to do much himself; A-kun's life wasn't in danger for now, so he'd let things take their course.

As it turned out, their calm seemed to do some good.

A-kun appeared satisfied that Gandalf wasn't making any moves or saying anything to him, and he drifted off toward the water, still naked and unsteady on his feet.

He seemed to stay high after that, but with someone always keeping an eye on him, he wandered the flower fields inside his head until, in the end, he came back to normal.

## Darkness

Once we'd made sure A-kun wasn't in such bad shape, I-chan and I headed back to the dance floor.

The dance floor was a fair distance away.

A ten-minute walk, maybe.

It was an inconvenient distance, but it was also what kept the chill-out area quiet.

About halfway between the chill-out area and the dance floor, everything went completely dark.

Naturally, there are no streetlights in the desert.

In the pitch dark, the ground that should have filled the lower half of the view was invisible, and the upper half glittered with stars.

Through all that, we walked toward the light and the sound.

It felt like the right road for a festival crossing from the twentieth century into the twenty-first.

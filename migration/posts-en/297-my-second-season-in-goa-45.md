## Goa in May

Y's DJ date was set for the middle of May.

Goa's high season ran from the end of December to the end of March, and once April came, most travelers moved north into the Himalayas.

Even the Goa-loving travelers who stubbornly hung on could not stand the heat by the end of April, and they headed north too.

Then May arrived, the monsoon right around the corner, and the only ones left were the true Goa people, the ones who lived there.

Nine Bar in mid-May was a party for exactly those seasoned Goa people, with almost no travelers in it.

Mostly old hippies in their forties to their sixties, tough old hands every one of them, the kind who had been traveling the world for decades.

It was only because so few people were left that a chance to DJ existed at all, and only because of that, the honor of playing in front of Goa's heavyweights had come to him.

## His Big Night

Landing the chance took Y's overconfident nature all the way to abnormal, and he was glowing with pride.

He seemed satisfied at having pulled ahead in the competition that ran among friends in Goa without anyone being aware of it: "who is the most intense traveler."

The rest of us, his closest friends, saw our moment and backed him with everything we had.

Y himself hyped it to every friend he had — you absolutely have to come to Nine Bar that day — setting up the perfect stage for his big night.

And so, in those perfect conditions, the day of the DJ set arrived.

## Expressing the Fire

Which track to play in which situation, how to mix from one kind of track into another, what kind of arc to build and how to bring it to an end.

Meticulously, leaving nothing out, he had every preparation in place before he took the stage.

Plenty of his non-Japanese friends turned up as well.

The only Japanese friends there were us, the close ones.

Everyone else had already left Goa.

Up on stage, once the first track was running, Y openly took out his prized chillum and smoked the finest cream — top-grade hash resin — he had been saving for this day.

Everything was going smoothly.

Filled to the brim with fire, and meaning to express that fire in the best DJ set he could deliver, Y took a large amount of LSD right there on stage.

He seemed to think that a boost from LSD would let him reach a higher dimension of DJ expression.

But unfortunately (obviously), his way of expressing that fire was badly wrong, and it took things somewhere outrageous.

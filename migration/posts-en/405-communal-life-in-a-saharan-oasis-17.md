## Stories Around the Campfire 1

When travelers get together, nothing gets people going like travel stories.

Everyone's interested, and everyone has a story of some kind.

The one that caught my interest was where D and E were headed next.

They weren't going back to where they'd come from, and they weren't staying on in Morocco, either.

They were going to India.

## The Maha Kumbh Mela

In a few weeks, India would be holding the Maha Kumbh Mela, the biggest festival in the world, which comes around once every twelve years, and that was where they were going.

Twelve years?!

The sheer scale of it blew me away.

The last one had been twelve years earlier, and the next wouldn't come for another twelve.

The festival we'd just been to was a once-in-a-hundred-years celebration of the turn of the century, and here they were, heading straight from that to a once-in-twelve-years festival. These two really were in another league.

Apparently tens of millions of people, or something like that, would gather there from all over the world.

Ever since they'd first heard about it, they said, they had been waiting for this moment.

Hearing all this, I got fired up for the road myself: All right, then, I'll make it to the next Maha Kumbh Mela, twelve years from now!

## Inspiration

Their hunger for travel was a huge inspiration to me.

One big event, then straight on to the next big event.

Live like that for years and you'd pile up an enormous amount of experience, which went a long way toward explaining that fierce aura of theirs.

They left me with a grand ambition of my own: to keep traveling as hungrily as they did, chasing one intense experience after another, and grow into a bigger person.

Inspiration that strong will change your personality whether you like it or not.

At just twenty-two, I was impressionable, and when I ran into personalities as intense as theirs, I had no way to resist.

Y and I talked it over later, and we agreed that to someone living as ferociously as D, a pair of travelers just starting out like us probably didn't even register.

Their aura was that otherworldly.

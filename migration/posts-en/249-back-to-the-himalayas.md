## The Reason

The reason I went back to Vashisht was partly to take it easy in the hot springs, and partly to stock up on high-grade hash for myself.

The time before, moving it from Vashisht down to Delhi, I had hidden it so well that there was never the slightest sign of anyone finding it, and carrying it on to Thailand had been just as easy, which had done a great deal for my confidence.

I had found a rebellious pleasure in taking the risk and taking the gain.

Making money this way was never something I meant to make a habit of, but a lot of the travelers I met around that time had rubbed off on me — people who flew around the world chasing psytrance parties.

Every one of them was a striking individual, full of life, shining as a human being.

No two of them were alike, but what they shared was a determination to live the best life they could, and the nerve to take real risks to get there.

That seriousness, that momentum, was not something you ever ran into living in ordinary society, and each of them was using every bit of the charisma they had.

Under that influence, I rode through the night back into the Himalayas to get my hands on the very best hash.

## The Mountains

Back in Vashisht after a few weeks away, the number of travelers had dropped off sharply and the village felt a little deserted.

The cold was harsher now, and the snow lying over the distant mountains had grown deeper.

I decided on the hut higher up the mountain, the one with the stove, where I had stayed the last time.

The idea was to light a fire in the stove and take it easy for a while, smoking top-grade hash.

After lazing about for a while and getting my preparations perfectly in order, I went back down to Delhi.

This time I meant to go straight south in one shot, all the way to Goa.

In Goa there would be a reunion with my close friend Y, and before long my girlfriend I-chan would be coming out from Japan.

What sort of Goa season it was going to be, I was already looking forward to finding out.

## The Move

On the last day of our lease on the hut, a truck came.

It was there to haul the everyday things we'd been using to Tangier, where Gandalf lived.

Every one of us had been in Gandalf's care from beginning to end.

It was a bit like being allowed to play in the palm of the Buddha's hand.

We cleaned the hut from top to bottom and got it ready to hand back to the locals.

I felt grateful, too, to the local people who had let a bunch of oddballs like us use their hut.

## Our Things

One by one, we loaded our things onto the truck.

Every piece of gear had its own memories packed into it.

Everyone loaded their things onto the truck, the people riding along climbed aboard, and everything was set for the trip.

Looking at it again, it wasn't much of a load.

We were all traveling out of backpacks, so nobody had more than the bare minimum.

## Goodbyes

For most of us, this was where we parted ways.

I would see Gandalf and F again later in Tangier, but I had no idea when I'd next see any of the others.

What came after, in all our lives, was completely uncharted territory.

We thanked each other, celebrated our friendship, and none of us wanted to say goodbye.

Meeting them had been nothing short of wonderful, and I was grateful for such a rare experience.

I'd never known that living out in nature with friends could be this much fun.

I like to think each of us grew, and pushed out the edges of what our lives could be.

The only real shame was that R had ended up in the role of the troublemaker.

She'd been a handful, but she was still one of the people we'd shared that time with, a comrade we had challenged, and been challenged by, in one scene of our lives.

The truck pulled out, headed for town.

Thinking back on the time we'd spent together, we kept waving until it was out of sight.

## Rise and Fall

On top of that, the boom in Goa the year before had drawn Indian businessmen who caught the scent of money, and they set about developing the place, and at the same time Indian tourists had started arriving in large numbers.

Most of them were rich boys from Bombay, spending big and partying loud, but they had none of the sharp edge that travelers have, none of that hunger, and Goa felt commercialized, cheapened.

All in all, last year's miraculous peak had been exactly that — a miracle, once and only once, not something anyone could recreate over and over.

That mood had spread across the whole of Goa, and a vague sense of letdown hung in the air.

But the only ones picking up on it were the people who had lived through the freakish peak of last year; to anyone who did not know, Goa was a tropical paradise.

And that only deepened the letdown for the ones who knew last year's Goa.

Because that ultimate, life-changing atmosphere was not there, and it was never something you could put into words.

Goa this year was an ordinary tropical tourist spot and nothing more, not the bewitched place that had opened a door to another dimension the year before.

That was exactly why Y had lost his inspiration, why he was down, why he was brooding.

## The Worry

His worry was not only that inspiration would not come; it reached back to the year before, to leaving Goa early, ahead of the end of the season.

Y had torn himself away from Goa for work that year, and he had come to see that in doing so he had missed a miracle that could never happen again.

Missing the greatest moment of your life for a job is a shameful thing for a Goa person, someone whose whole life is staked on partying like it might kill him, and it is not something you can easily make peace with.

He had come back to Goa as prepared as a man could be, to make up for that regret, but with the inspiration gone there was nothing to be done.

At the same time, he had begun to worry that choosing Goa over South Africa for the greatest moment of the millennium might have been a mistake.

We had all gathered around Y's film project, and with his worry at the center, the gears were about to start slipping.

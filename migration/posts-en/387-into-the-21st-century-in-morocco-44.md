## Janis's Party

Janis's ten-ton truck, it turned out, had company: a couple traveling in a camper was riding along with it.

They didn't have the untamed personalities of Janis and her wild boyfriend; if anything, they were hippie travelers with an intellectual look about them.

At the after-party, the boyfriend in that couple started DJing.

The intellectual look apparently wasn't just for show; it came through in his DJing, too.

The moment he put on a track, the air changed.

## Refinement

They seemed to be the ultimate psytrance travelers, refined down to the last detail.

The speakers they traveled with were astonishingly good, clear and delicate yet carrying a deep, booming bass.

The speakers at the festival had sounded decent even at full volume, but they were nothing compared to the ones this couple had brought.

The sound seeped right into you.

He was a complete unknown as a DJ, yet his skills were plainly among the best in the world.

Instead of building a career in the DJ world, he traveled with Janis, putting on parties around Europe and Africa and living for the fun of it.

Maybe it was that charmed life, but his talent had come into full bloom, and his set was superb.

The DJing, the track selection, the speakers: all of it was top-notch, and the famous DJs and giant speakers at the festival paled beside it.

## The Dancers

Dancing at this top-quality after-party were travelers with time on their hands, people who had decided to stay on after the festival ended.

Only the most intense, most interesting characters were left.

It was as if yokai, the monsters out of Japanese folk tales, were gathering in the desert night after night for a secret party.

The after-party blew me away.

I'd had a fantastic time at the festival, but somewhere in the back of my mind I'd been comparing it to the parties in Goa, and I'd never quite enjoyed it all the way.

This after-party swept that pent-up dissatisfaction clean away.

It was an insanely fun party, and an insanely high-quality one.

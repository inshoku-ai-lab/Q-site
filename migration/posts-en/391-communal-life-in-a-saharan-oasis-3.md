## The Gear

Gandalf hadn't brought only people. A whole load of gear came with them.

Rice, wheat, vegetables and other food; the carpets and cushions from the festival; cookware, carpentry tools, and so on.

The biggest and most eye-catching of it all was a huge metal object.

Mounted on top was a metal dish like a satellite dish, more than a meter (over three feet) across, and it could pivot on the base underneath.

At the point where the dish focused, there was a metal frame that seemed to serve some purpose.

It's hard to describe in words, so take a look at the picture.

## The Doctor of Ecology

I had no idea what the thing was for, so I asked Gandalf.

It was a solar cooker, he said. It reflected the sun's heat onto a single point, and you could cook with it.

He'd built it himself.

That was when we found out that Gandalf had once been active as a doctor of ecology and had been involved in things like developing solar cells.

He had all sorts of inventions, apparently.

So the old wizard didn't conjure things up with magic. He'd been inventing them with science.

## Landing

Our new friends carried their things into the hut, and each of them found a spot inside to settle into.

Every one of them was smiling, full of anticipation.

The general feeling was: no idea what this is, but it looks like fun, so why not.

In fact, nobody had any idea how things would play out from here.

Probably not even Gandalf, who'd organized the whole thing.

Which is exactly what makes an adventure fun.

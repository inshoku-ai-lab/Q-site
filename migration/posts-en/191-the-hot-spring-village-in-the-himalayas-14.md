## A Party in the Himalayas

It had gone dark by now, and the sky was filled from edge to edge with stars.

Clean air and high altitude — the number of stars was nothing you could compare to the sky over Osaka.

After a long, long wait, the sound came on.

The whole place broke into whoops and strange yells, as if that was what everyone had been waiting for.

We had timed it for this moment: all of us dropped LSD at once and lifted off together for another dimension.

The speakers were far smaller than the ones at parties in Goa or Japan, but they were more than enough to make us happy.

I gave myself over to the rhythm and the melody and traveled into the world of sound.

Since Goa, where I first found out how good a trance party could be, my head had been nothing but trance, and after I left, all of it stayed bottled up.

I went to parties in Japan, but they came nowhere near satisfying me; what my soul wanted was the extreme craziness that only India has.

Here, finally, I was filled with the feeling that I had come back to where I belonged.

## The Cast

On the scale of craziness, the party in Manali was without question at the very top.

Traveling in India in the first place already puts you outside ordinary common sense, and coming up to Manali puts you off the usual traveler's route as well.

And to climb two hours up a mountain, get wrecked on LSD, and keep dancing all night deep in the hills is not the act of a sane person.

All of us enjoyed that craziness, and we were proud of having stepped outside society and made our own fun.

And people like that gathered here from all over the world.

The cast at this party was quite a lineup.

There were plenty of people who stood out, but the first one to catch your eye was the Greek woman who lived in Vashisht.

Tall and beautiful, like a model, she had cut herself completely loose from common sense, and the way she danced was unreal.

For all her model's looks, she danced in a way no one could call beautiful or cool, not even out of flattery.

She would leap into the air out of nowhere, then drop into a bow-legged squat, then run around and spin in circles.

It was magnificent self-expression, the kind that overturns the whole concept of dance.

You could tell at a glance that she had arrived at this after years of expressing herself at trance parties.

Her free, immense energy was a blessing on the whole party.

## The Downward Spiral

Everything, all of it, was turning in a downward spiral.

The biggest cause, I suspect, was the fixation Y and I had on bringing in the millennium at the greatest party there was.

On top of that, I think, the loop in our heads that kept comparing last year's Goa to this year's Goa twisted everything out of shape, the childishness in us blamed other people for problems that were our own, and those feelings and the actions that came out of them turned into a game of passing blame back and forth that split us apart.

Even if the parties had lost their momentum, Goa was still a tropical paradise, and interesting people still came together there from all over the world.

Even if it was no longer the best place in the world for us, it was still an environment interesting enough to rank in the world's top five, and we were letting it go to waste.

The downward spiral was not just ours; it reached the many travelers who came to Goa for the parties.

With the parties not taking off, the drug dealers could not sell what they had, and the travelers running stalls at the flea market were lamenting their falling sales.

That atmosphere brought discord even among the Japanese Goa people who had long been friends.

Being around people who were not enjoying their days was never going to be fun, and it could not be helped that the distance between us widened on its own.

## January in Goa

In Goa, the parties stop from the middle of January to the middle of February.

That is because the local schools go into their exam season, and loud noise in the middle of the night gets in the way of the students' studying.

For me, the focus shifting away from the parties was a rescue.

It meant I could set aside the wish that was never going to come true — getting wrecked at a party and cutting loose — and shift my focus onto enjoying daily life.

With that, my criticism of I-chan softened, I found I could accept her, and I started to enjoy my days.

At the same time the headaches that had dragged on and on began to clear, and little by little the smile came back to my strained face.

## The After-After-Party

The after-party, which had been an absolute blast, was over, and I was going on excitedly with my Japanese friend Y about just how amazing it had been when Gandalf came over.

He wore an even more serious look than usual.

Once the festival was over, he planned to gather some friends and live at the oasis, and he wanted to know if we'd like to come.

He'd already rented something like a storage shed, and he said it would work as a shelter.

The plan was open-ended; if things went well, he said, he might just stay on and live there.

Now that was a surprise.

And since the invitation came from Gandalf, of all people, a man I looked up to, there was no way I could say no.

I had no idea what exactly we'd be doing, but it was bound to be interesting.

Conveniently, we had no plans whatsoever for what came next.

That kind of freedom is the privilege of the young: no money, but plenty of time.

Y's plans were apparently open-ended too, and he jumped at the chance to join.

For someone like him, whose travels doubled as a research trip for his writing, it was the perfect opportunity.

## The Hotel

We decided to head back to the hotel for a while once the festival ended.

But this wasn't the end, just an intermission.

We'd assumed the celebration would be over when the festival was, so this turn of events was almost too good to be true.

The fun would keep going, and we'd get to spend time with Gandalf.

News this good doesn't come along very often.

After a few days of rest, Gandalf came to pick us up, fully prepared.

We were taking a shared taxi out to the oasis.

A new adventure was coming right on the heels of the last one, and our hearts were racing.

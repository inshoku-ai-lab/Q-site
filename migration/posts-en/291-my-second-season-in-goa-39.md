## Back to Being a Baby

I lay with my head in my girlfriend I-chan's lap, looking around at everything that was going on.

I could see everything around me, and I could hear the conversations and understand them, but I had no judgment about it at all.

No opinions of my own, no criticism, no praise.

I just looked at all of it exactly as it was, and accepted it.

Even while I watched it calmly, I was aware that I was regressing into a baby.

I understood perfectly well that what I was at that moment was a baby: able to do nothing at all, easily hurt, innocent.

And I had dumped the care of my body and my mind entirely onto my friends.

Whether I lived or died was completely up to them.

I was twenty-one; my friends were around twenty-nine.

Without realizing it, I was getting the absolute maximum out of being the baby of the group.

## A Day to Remember

Every one of my friends at that party was more wrecked than they had ever been, no longer belonging to the human world at all.

We resonated with one another at a high vibration from another dimension, sharing a world none of us had known.

All of us understood that this was the day we had opened the door to another dimension, a day worth marking, and all of us were grateful that we got to spend that moment together.

And at that moment of great harmony, instead of mixing the track he was playing into the next one, the DJ let it run all the way to the end.

The party was over.

In Goa that year the law had tightened, and music could be played only until ten at night.

After that you were a criminal, and you went to prison.

## A Chance

A huge wave of applause and cheering, breaking out the instant the music stopped.

For everyone there, the party seemed to have been the best experience of their lives, and neither the applause nor the cheering would stop.

But not one of us was ready for it to end.

None of us had gotten this high with any thought for what came after.

We had gone all the way out with everything we had, body and soul.

Letting the party end like this would be far too much of a waste.

Above all, everyone understood that this would probably be the last open-air party of the year, and that this special moment would never come back.

The one who stood up then was Y, the hot-blooded one.

"I'll take care of this," he said, and worked his way into the conversation between the DJs and the police who had come to stop the party.

## Bamboo Forest

The party was being held inside a bamboo grove called Bamboo Forest.

We got there to find the place packed, in spite of how near the end of the season it was.

Everyone there was deep Goa — no passing tourists, no beginners who hadn't learned the place yet.

Just knowing that a crowd of crazy travelers was gathered this far into the jungle getting wrecked was enough to lift me.

Everyone else seemed to feel the same. Every face in the place was lit up.

My Japanese friends had already assembled, taken over one of the chai stalls, and had a chillum going around.

Laughter and grinning spreading out from them. The party was already set up nicely.

## More

The acid I'd taken twenty minutes earlier had started working. My vision was bending, the music was bending, and I was stepping over into somewhere other-dimensional.

Which is what happens in twenty minutes when you take eight times the normal dose.

I went to the dance floor and moved to the music.

Normally, Goa trance is the kind of music that hauls your consciousness in and makes you dance whether you meant to or not, and by the time you notice, you're dancing like a lunatic.

But that night my body wasn't going with it.

I was wound as tight as I could be and wanted to be dancing that second, and my body wouldn't follow.

So I decided to add half a tab of Hofmann on top.

I wasn't going to waste my last chance at a party. I intended to go all the way out.

Eight times the normal dose already in me, another eight on top — sixteen times a normal dose of acid in total.

It was absurd, and the risk I was carrying was enormous, the kind where your mind goes out and doesn't come back. But at twenty-one, on a roll, I couldn't see risk at all.

Don't waste the last chance. Enjoy it to the absolute limit.

That was the whole of it, and I went in at full speed.

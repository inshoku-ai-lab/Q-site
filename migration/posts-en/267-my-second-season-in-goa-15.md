## After the New Year's Party

We went to Y's house and unwound with a chillum.

After a while we got hungry, so we all decided to break into the mochi — pounded rice cakes — that Y had carried all the way from Japan specially for this day.

Japanese ingredients are hard to come by in India, and to grill mochi at New Year's, wrap it in nori seaweed, and dip it in soy sauce is an outrageous luxury.

Just how special that is may be hard to grasp unless you have traveled India for a long stretch, but carrying mochi in a backpack with only so much room in it — mochi that is heavy, and that is gone the moment you eat it — is well outside the ordinary.

The mochi was good, and once it was gone and we had caught our breath, we were finally ready to hear what Y had to say.

By Y's account, he had grasped the secret of life.

But it was not a thing that could be put into words, and he kept repeating, "No, that's not it — but that's it."

In the end nobody could work out what Y meant, and we gave up.

Right to the end Y was all confidence: "I'll prove it to you."

## The Change in A-san

Fired up by Y's awakening, something inside A-san came loose.

There was a good deal of history behind how it got to that point, and the same thing that had been happening with me and I-chan — finding her uncool, finding her irritating — was happening between Y and A-san as well.

In the world of the Goa travelers, nothing was valued more than being crazy, being off the rails, having good taste, being cool.

That standard of cool included things like having a lot of travel behind you, and being fun to be around.

A-san was a man from the film industry, on the cool side of Japanese society, but once he came into the world of the Goa travelers he got treated as an amateur with no travel behind him.

Unable to bear being treated that way, A-san pushed himself to act like a veteran, which only made him painful to watch, and Y found A-san irritating.

And A-san, feeling Y put a little distance between them, tried harder still, which made him more painful to watch still... and a vicious cycle was under way.

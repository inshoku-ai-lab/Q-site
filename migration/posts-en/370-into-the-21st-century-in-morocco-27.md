## First Time

The Malana cream D had brought called for a chillum worthy of it, but he hadn't brought one, so as not to risk getting caught at customs.

So when I shared the chillum I'd brought from India, he was delighted.

For my part, it was an honor to win the approval of a big-league traveler with as many years on the road as D.

Following the etiquette of India's sadhus, we passed the chillum around the circle.

The smoke drew surprisingly light, and then a gentle, deep high came on.

And it tasted unbelievably good.

The rumors about Malana cream weren't just rumors. They were true.

Of all my friends, I think I was the first to try the real thing.

Seen that way, I was proud of this unusual experience of mine.

## D's Treasure

But the Malana cream wasn't the only remarkable thing.

A while after we'd smoked the chillum together, D shared something else: liquid LSD.

What usually goes around is scraps of paper soaked in the liquid, but D apparently got his straight from the source, so it was still liquid.

Given D's gravitas, and the fact that he had Malana cream, I'd imagined his LSD would be something special too, but the real thing went far beyond anything I'd pictured.

With ordinary LSD, once you take it, your mind revs up and won't settle; you're a little confused, but inspiration comes to you in waves.

It's good for dancing hard, for enjoying psychedelic visions and auditory hallucinations, for wandering through a world of inspired consciousness, but not for calm conversation or relaxing and resting.

I believed that was simply what LSD was.

At least, that was how it had gone in the dozens of times I'd taken it.

But D's stuff was in another dimension altogether.

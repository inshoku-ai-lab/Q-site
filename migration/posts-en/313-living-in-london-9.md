## The Weekend Psytrance Party

The weekend I got hold of LSD through T's connection, I headed straight for a psytrance party.

Readers who have been following the paid posts of this travelogue may be thinking that it is nothing but drugs and parties, on and on — and that is exactly right.

Back then I was deep into psytrance parties, LSD, and cannabis.

That was what my life actually revolved around, and what interested me more than anything else.

Saturday night I finished work, met I-chan at the station, and we headed for the psytrance club.

In Goa it had been outdoor parties and nothing else, and indoor club parties held no interest for me at all, but in London I was in no position to be picky.

The place we had come to was a fairly well-known underground club called the Drome, and the people it drew were wildly uninhibited — outlandish and strange and one of a kind.

Sure enough, the way psytrance and LSD together draw strange people in and multiply their peculiarities many times over seemed to be the same everywhere in the world.

## The Fire Show

We were waiting our turn to get in when some people started up a fire show for the line.

They called out a loud greeting to everyone, then calmly set their staffs alight and started spinning them around and around.

Nobody had imagined they would suddenly be watching a fire show in the middle of the city like this, and everybody was delighted; the whole line came alive.

When the show was over, one of the performers came around with a hat to collect change.

This, apparently, was how they made their living.

Maybe they were living in a squat house somewhere?

## A-kun

I hadn't noticed during the fire show, but once it was over and I got a good look, one of them was an Asian guy.

He looked about my own age, with a head of short dreadlocks.

I'd had dreads myself in India, but coming to London I had combed them out so I could blend into city life, and gone to long straight hair.

He looked Japanese, so I tried a "Konnichiwa" on him, and sure enough he was — it surprised him, but he answered with a smile.

One look and each of us could see the other was living the same kind of life, and we hit it off in an instant.

## A-kun, Vacant

I-chan and I both felt close to A-kun and thought of him as a friend.

But the only languages we could speak were Japanese and English that was barely more than broken, and the only ones A-kun spoke were Moroccan and French (Morocco used to be a French colony).

In other words, we had no language in common, and gestures were all we had to communicate with.

That was where things stood when we came across A-kun, wandering around stark naked.

Another friend rushed over and tried to wrap a blanket around him, but he threw it back, wanting nothing of the kind.

In a situation like this, the custom is for the closest friend to look after the person.

## Rejection

Here, that meant Gandalf would be the one taking care of A-kun.

He had known A-kun since he was a little boy and had looked after him many times.

For this festival in particular, A-kun's parents had entrusted him to Gandalf's care.

Naturally, it fell to Gandalf.

But A-kun, whose deep consciousness had come to the surface and exploded, refused to let Gandalf look after him.

Apparently, being told to do this and do that all through the build-up to the festival had been stressing him out.

Now that his mind had gone back to a baby's, whatever had been holding him in check gave way, and he simply refused.

Whenever Gandalf came near, he pulled a face and ran off.

## Bewilderment

This left Gandalf thoroughly bewildered too.

He had looked after A-kun with the best of intentions, and even now, in an emergency with a thousand things to do, he was going out of his way to take care of him, but there's nothing you can do when the person himself won't have it.

Gandalf, by this point, was also completely wrecked on the top-grade LSD D had supplied.

To be that far gone and still take something this tangled calmly in stride, the man had a gravitas that was something else.

D and E, for their part, also looked on at A-kun's naked confusion without turning a hair.

I suppose they were more or less used to it.

The composure and gravitas each of them had was something else.

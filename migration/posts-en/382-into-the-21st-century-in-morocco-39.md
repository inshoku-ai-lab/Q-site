## LSD and Flipping Out

Taking LSD and flipping out happens a lot. To people who are used to it, it's the same old story.

In 99.9 percent of cases, you come back to everyday life as the drug wears off.

It's only in very rare cases that someone doesn't come back: when they've mixed several kinds of drugs, when they had a predisposition to mental illness to begin with, or when they were in a truly unusual setting.

Take a reasonable dose of good-quality LSD from a known source, with understanding friends around you, and it's not much of a problem.

It mostly happens to people who haven't been taking LSD for long.

Some call it a bad trip, but it's also an experience that goes beyond simple good and bad.

## A Rite of Passage

Most people with LSD experience have been through this state, and it's a rite of passage of sorts.

All at once you meet a deep layer of consciousness that was inside you all along, and you're made to face deep questions about life that had never crossed your mind.

Along the way, overwhelmed by more information than your consciousness can process, you flip out.

For A-kun, too, it may have been a rite of passage on the way to standing on his own as an adult.

## Wandering Naked

A-kun was confused by his first LSD experience and had lost all sense of what was going on.

The fact that he didn't know what was going on panicked him even more, a state something like a panic disorder.

Lost and bewildered, with his conscious mind gone off somewhere, A-kun regressed to something like an infant's state of mind, took off all his clothes, and started wandering around naked.

That was the state he was in, wandering naked, when we met him that day.

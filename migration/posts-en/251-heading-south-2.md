## Killing Time

To avoid the usual barrage of questions from Indians, I had taken the top berth in the second-class sleeper car.

Up there, whenever it got to be a hassle, I could stretch out on the bunk with earphones in and music playing, and they would leave me alone.

To get through the boredom of a ride that long, I had a trick ready.

Eating hash.

Swallow a small amount along with something greasy, and about an hour later the cannabis comes creeping on.

The effect lasts a long time, so as it wore off I topped it up, and went on with the train ride at my leisure.

Over all those hours, the only thing I did was stay wrecked and keep listening to music.

Listening to music under the influence of cannabis is a very different thing from listening in ordinary consciousness, and it never got old.

There was always something new in it, the music always felt good, and it was always full of inspiration.

## The Kashmiri

Before I left Delhi, a friendly young man who said he was from Kashmir struck up a conversation with me.

Kashmir is the region in India's northwest that borders Pakistan and Afghanistan.

We hit it off from that first exchange and drank chai together.

Before long the conversation turned to hash, and then to how he had good stuff — did I want to buy some?

Shouldering a bit more risk, carrying it down to Goa and turning it into money — that did not seem like a bad idea, so I decided to at least hear him out.

I walked along behind him and into a souvenir shop on the main street.

Inside the shop they brought out chai, but I had heard stories of sleeping pills being slipped into it in situations like this, so I turned it down, just in case.

They were drinking the same chai, though, so on second thought I decided there was no need to worry, and drank it with them.

## The Deal

I waited a while over the chai, and they brought out Kashmiri hash from the back of the shop.

I said it smelled a little strange, and they said that was simply how the Kashmiri stuff smells.

I smoked some, and it was nothing special, but it wasn't bad either, and weighed against the price it seemed like a fair enough deal.

So I bought about five hundred grams (a bit over a pound), and that was a huge mistake.

I'll get to that story later.

## A Note Written on April 24, 2021

There was a reason I went on and on about how grateful I am to Gandalf in the last post.

Today, for the first time in a very long time, I opened Gandalf's Facebook page.

Part of it was nostalgia, since I've been writing about him almost every day, and I'd also been thinking about taking a trip to Morocco soon — and if I went, I very much wanted to see him again.

So I opened his page, and to my shock, I found that Gandalf had died a month ago.

That was right around the time I was posting the story of how the festival in Morocco turned out, exactly when my mind was on him.

Of course, it has hit me hard.

They say you don't know what you've got till it's gone, and I'm feeling the full weight of those words.

The time I spent with Gandalf ran from the end of 2000 into the beginning of 2001.

More than ten years after that, we connected on Facebook.

I was happy about it, but I felt a little shy, and I also wanted to put real, offline connections ahead of online ones on Facebook, so Gandalf and I never really talked much.

Twenty years ago I couldn't speak English, so we couldn't have a conversation; ten years ago, once I could, I should have talked with him more.

But I think the shyness was the bigger part of it, and I never really reached out, all the way up to now.

And then, just a few hours ago, I looked at Facebook and got the shock.

A little while ago, I lit incense and held a ceremony of my own to send his soul off, gave thanks for his life, and resolved to carry on his legacy, and now I feel calm.

Having reaffirmed that his soul will live on in my heart, I also feel as if I've renewed my resolve to live this life properly.

Whatever else, Gandalf was an enormous presence in my life.

I want to keep letting him influence me for the better.

## One-Way Street

Every one of us shared the same feeling: there was no going back.

That was how overwhelming the Goa Gil experience at Christmas had been.

Once you cross the line and blow wide open, the blade doesn't go back into the sheath it came out of.

School and society spend years tidying your individuality into order and fitting it inside a frame; an experience strong enough expands it, and it no longer fits the frame.

Of course, Goa Gil knows this will happen, and he hands people the opportunity anyway.

Entirely premeditated.

## Shock

Understanding in my bones that I could never go back was a big shock.

I had come all the way to Goa wanting an experience big enough to change my life, and now the size of it left me at a loss.

For better or worse, I hadn't had a stable life since dropping out of high school, so there was no sheath for me to go back into, and that made it easier.

My travel money wasn't infinite; a few more months and it would be gone.

Given how much I had changed, I tried to think about what kind of life to live back in Japan, but digesting a shift that big took everything, and no real thinking came out of it.

The present was simply too much fun for any anxiety about the future to take hold, and nothing outside this moment interested me anymore.

## Thinking

I smoked joints every day, and by the time lunch came around at midday I was already high.

Being high on cannabis had become my normal state, and it was enjoyable enough that quitting never crossed my mind.

As long as I stayed high, though, I couldn't think through anything ahead of me properly, so I gave up thinking about the future.

Sooner or later the money would run out and I would leave Goa, and out of Goa there would be no joints and a clear head again — so until then, I decided, no thinking, just fun.

My days went into getting the most out of the moment I was in.

## Nothing but Parties

Anyone who was going to parties in Goa over the New Year from 1998 into 1999 probably couldn't do anything but go to parties.

The best parties were coming one after another, and simply getting through days that fun took everything a person had.

After the huge Christmas party ended, we rested until our bodies had recovered, and the day after that, the next big party was already set up.

It's a similar story, so I'll skip the details, but that party too was as fun and as charged as it gets, and it expanded my consciousness and gave me another big experience of life.

With each round I could feel myself changing at tremendous speed.

Until then I had existed inside the frame society built; now the frame was torn away, and it felt like I had been handed infinite possibility.

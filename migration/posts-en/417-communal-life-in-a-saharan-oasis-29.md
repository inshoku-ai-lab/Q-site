## Ecological Fundamentalism

His thinking on ecology went a little too far in places, and some people might see it as a kind of fundamentalism.

Hidden in it are the traps of communist thinking, the global warming scam, and so on, but in these chronicles I'm deliberately leaving all that alone.

If you're curious, look through the information articles I've written.

What I learned from Gandalf was how badly humanity had been polluting the Earth all along, and how much the fate of this planet could change depending on how careful we are.

Above all, I learned not to use plastic products.

Once plastic has been made, it's bound to be thrown away sooner or later.

And when that time comes, there is no way to get rid of it without polluting the Earth.

To be precise, bacteria that eat plastic have since been discovered, and it's now possible to dispose of it without polluting the environment, but back in 2001 nobody knew about that.

## Nature

Gandalf taught me what nature is, and what it means to live with it.

I knew nothing at all about those things, but once I knew, I found them irresistible.

Stay with nature, and everything works out.

People talk about nature as a threat, but what Gandalf taught me was that nature is kind.

I came to see that nature isn't an enemy to be conquered, but an ally that gently protects us.

## How He Taught

Gandalf didn't teach those ideas about ecology in an academic way, or in a preachy one; he slipped them in casually, in the middle of daily life and everyday conversation.

I was a young traveler bursting with defiance. If he had preached at me, I would have pushed back on the spot and learned nothing.

Gandalf knew exactly how to teach young hippie travelers.

## F Cuts Loose and the Party Baba

F, who had taken the strong LSD, kept whooping it up with the party energy of a jock.

Riding that momentum, he cut loose, dancing hard in front of the speakers, until he collided with that same party baba, the one who was trouble.

Normally, bumping into someone while dancing at a party is common enough that the two of you smile, apologize, and that's the end of it, but F had picked the wrong man.

The party baba was a rough character, and he glared at F.

F could do nothing about the glare, so he kept dancing, but it had brought his mood down, and he decided to leave the dance floor.

Then, as bad luck would have it, the party baba had stepped out for a break at the same moment, and they collided again in the hallway, and F got another glare.

Fogged by the LSD, F bristled at the glare and raised his voice, but there was no way he was going to beat a party baba who had trained as a sadhu and survived among India's party travelers, where anything, however insane, can happen, and the baba roared back at him even louder.

## A Fight in a Hallucination

With things now hostile between him and the party baba, F's LSD high climbed toward its peak, and at the strongest point of it he went into a bad trip.

The bad blood between them held, and from a little way off, the party baba fired a Kamehameha at F.

Readers of this blog may be thinking, what is he talking about, but I mean it literally: the party baba fired a Kamehameha.

Some of you might get there faster if I said Hadouken, but either way it's that move out of the manga, palms out, a wave of energy.

F, his mind fogged and deep in a bad trip, took that literal Kamehameha literally.

Blown off his feet by the Kamehameha, F went down with the force of it.

His arms were wet with sweat from dancing, but caught in the hallucination of the bad trip, F took it for blood.

The hallucination that the damage from the Kamehameha had him bleeding from head to toe.

Apparently he was actually feeling the pain, too.

Battered by the Kamehameha and close to death, F barely made it out with his life, found B, and asked for help.

## The Next Day

The day after my second DMT session, the fateful day arrived.

In Goa everyone was going through some experience of their own that changed their life, and Goa the year before had already changed the way I looked at life, but this time a day of transformation on a much bigger scale had arrived.

The sky had been threatening all morning.

Heavy gray cloud covered the sky, with lightning flashing in the distance.

The high season of Goa's dry months was ending and shifting over into the rainy season — a time of transformation in itself.

That day we had gone over to Y's house to eat lunch together, and that was where the news reached us.

Right at that moment, a Goa trance party was under way at a venue in South Anjuna called Bamboo Forest.

All year the police had been shutting parties down, and there had not been a proper one in over a month.

Even when one did happen it was over by ten at night, but the first outdoor party in a long time still had us fired up.

It was mid-April, the Goa season winding down, and we accepted that this would be the last party of the season and swore to each other that we would send up the biggest fireworks of our lives.

## On the Way

I-chan and I decided to head home first to get ready for the party.

We put together a big supply of drinking water and more than enough charas and LSD.

The plan was to get wrecked all the way to the limit and go out on the best ending there was.

But this party would be over at ten p.m.

It was already past two in the afternoon.

Even if I took LSD that second, the party would end before the eight hours it lasts were up.

Somehow or other I wanted to enjoy the party right to the limit.

I-chan was driving the motorcycle that day, so from the back seat I pulled the plastic-wrapped LSD out of my bag and threw it into my mouth.

LSD takes about an hour to come on, and it would be close to thirty minutes before we reached the venue, so waiting was out of the question.

The party was over at ten at night, after all — if I didn't get myself flying right here and now, there was no enjoying it to the fullest.

Taking LSD while riding on a moving motorcycle did a great deal to build the momentum toward the high.

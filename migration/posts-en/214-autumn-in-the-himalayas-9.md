## My Best Friend from Goa

Y, who I had spent every single day with for three months or so back in Goa the last time I was in India, turned up in Vashisht.

He was working in the film business in Japan, steadily building toward Goa the following year.

He wanted to put that talent of his to work and shoot a documentary during the next Goa season.

The experience of Goa had been that special, and he had the passion and the talent to make it happen.

So he had been in Japan, working through his preparations one step at a time — until an email told him that our whole crowd of mutual friends, me included, was in Vashisht having the time of our lives, and he could not sit still, and he flew out to India.

The effort his dream demanded and the urge to be out enjoying himself with his friends right now pulled in opposite directions.

He found a way to force those two contradictory desires into one, which is why he abandoned the approach he had been taking and flew to India.

## Smuggling Cannabis

The method he had arrived at was to carry hash, one of Manali's local specialties, back to Japan, sell it at a high price, and raise the money for his travels that way.

Making money by smuggling hash was far too dangerous, and it carried the huge risk that if you got caught, every freedom you had would be stripped away — and at the same time it had a huge appeal, because it let you keep traveling by using society without ever joining it.

A sizable share of the travelers in India who were deep into psytrance made their living smuggling drugs.

Of the people who had spent years moving between the psytrance parties of Goa and Manali and Europe, more than half were funding their travels through drugs in one form or another.

At a glance they each looked like they had jobs of their own, but behind the scenes they were dealing in something or other, and every one of them had become part of that world.

At the bottom of it was this: either way you were going to be carrying cannabis and LSD on you at all times, so the risk was always there.

In which case, why not carry a little extra and make some money? That was the thinking.

## The Countryside

We left the city and headed for the countryside.

It was only about forty minutes' drive from town, but in just forty minutes the whole feel of the place changed.

From the urban life of a Moroccan city to a dry port town on the Mediterranean coast.

Our first stop was the home of a Moroccan hippie couple who lived nearby.

This time, in complete contrast to F's place, everything had a rustic, natural feel.

They were a calm couple in their fifties, and they made us tea with mint picked from their garden.

They didn't speak English, so we couldn't communicate much, but there was something about them that put us at ease.

You could tell just from their manner that they lived at an unhurried pace.

The contrast with F's life could hardly have been sharper, and it stayed with me.

## Gandalf's Place

We left their house and drove to where Gandalf lived.

F lived the city life, the Moroccan hippie couple lived the country life, and Gandalf, the nature lover, went a step further still.

He had leased part of a campground and built a dome on it to live in.

It was a round frame of wooden poles joined with iron connectors, covered in thick cotton cloth.

Morocco doesn't get much rain, so something that simple probably held up fine.

## Family

Gandalf and his family had apparently lived like this, close to nature, for years.

His two daughters, nine and eleven, were bright and sweet.

But he had divorced only about two months earlier, and the atmosphere was hard to call harmonious.

His wife seemed to find us a bit of a nuisance, and on the whole, the mood wasn't friendly.

Even so, we were happy to see how Gandalf, a man we looked up to, actually lived.

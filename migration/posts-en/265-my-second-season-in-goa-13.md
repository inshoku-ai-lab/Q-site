## Overdose

As a rule, when you take LSD your perception speeds up, your senses intensify, and your body moves faster.

But past a certain line, perception and senses and body can no longer keep up with that extreme activation, and they shut down.

Once you are in that state, what runs wild is not perception in the brain, not sensation in the nerves, not the physical body — only the spiritual part of consciousness.

To put it in a way only some people will get: it is Bucciarati after taking a hit from Gold Experience.

It is a very dangerous state, and for someone without the nerve for it, or someone who has never experienced the world of consciousness, going in means confusion and fear and a traumatic bad trip.

Most of the people traveling in India have the nerve, so they get over this wall, have an awakening, and enjoy it — but it is not a wall everybody gets over.

## Y's Challenge

At the New Year's party, Y meant to take a huge dose of LSD and cross that line.

A single tab of what they call paper acid, a scrap of paper about seven millimeters (a quarter inch) square, was one serving, but Y was a ripped martial artist, so a normal dose for him ran to about a tab and a half.

This time, though, he went for something reckless: four tabs of a variety called Hofmann, about twice as strong as ordinary LSD.

That came out to eight times the strength of a normal tab.

By Y's own scale, it was more than five times his usual dose.

Sure enough, Y, who had been dancing along fine, seized up partway through and could not move.

The symptoms of an overdose.

It was the same thing that had happened to me in Goa the year before, when an eighth of a tab was actually the right amount for me and I took a whole one and froze, unable to move.

It was Y's first time, and he seemed to be sinking deeper and deeper into consciousness.

I had learned from my own experience that a big dose of LSD does not necessarily add up to a dancing high at a party, so I took my eighth and held the line right at the edge, just short of an overdose.

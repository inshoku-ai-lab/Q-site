## The Waterfall with the Rainbow

We went straight on at a fast walk toward the sound of the falls.

Through gaps in the roar I could hear my friends' shouts of delight, but the waterfall itself was still out of sight.

It seemed to be just around the bend.

The moment the falls finally came into view, a cry of joy came out of me before I knew I was making it.

Part of it must have been the rainy season, but the amount of water thundering down that huge rock face was staggering.

The early-afternoon sun reflected off the full sheet of spray bursting up off it, forming an enormous rainbow.

An incredibly clear, sharp rainbow, and wrapped around it another one with the colors running in reverse.

A double rainbow!

But looking closer, outside that outer rainbow hung yet another, the colors flipped once more.

A miracle of the heavens — a triple rainbow was hanging there!

I had never once expected to round a bend and find a triple rainbow in front of me, and the beauty of it blew me away — which is how that cry of joy came out of me.

These rainbows weren't the half-circle shape you see in town; they were perfect full circles.

The triple circle of rainbow gave off a mysterious glow, and the grandeur of the scene left us dumbstruck.

## The Chillum

Enormous boulders lay scattered beside the falls.

We sat in a circle on one that was big and flat.

In front of us, the giant waterfall and the rainbows; around us, close friends.

Everyone was wrecked on LSD, shaking at the combination — a dreamlike world out of another dimension set against a view so real, so overwhelmingly there.

To settle that intense experience of the real and take our time celebrating it, we passed a chillum around the circle.

In the sadhu culture of India, smoking cannabis from a chillum in a circle was a sacred ritual, an act that celebrated your connection to other people and to the universe.

Fiercely strong hash and tobacco came through the chillum and down into my lungs.

For some reason, when you're on LSD, cannabis smoke moves into your body more smoothly than usual.

The two must go well together.

The tension of the LSD and the looseness of the cannabis mixed together, and in that comfortable altered state I sat with my friends and watched the falls and the rainbows.

It was a moment of pure bliss.

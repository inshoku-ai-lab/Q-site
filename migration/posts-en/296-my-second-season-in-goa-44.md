## Y's Dream

My closest friend Y came out of the film world, where he had worked as an assistant director.

A year and a half earlier he had come to India to travel, discovered Goa, and had his life changed at the root; he wanted to make a documentary film out of the Goa miracle, and he had come back to Goa with every last preparation perfectly in place.

But Goa that year had lost its energy for any number of reasons, his inspiration fell off sharply, and he threw out the plan to shoot the film.

What took hold of his inspiration instead was the music that was the biggest reason any of us were in Goa at all: Goa trance.

A lot of people who fall for Goa trance dream at least once of becoming a Goa trance DJ.

A DJ who put on cool music and made everyone dance, who became a star on his own talent and got fussed over for it, was what everybody wanted to be.

At a Goa trance party, where everyone who gathered was confident in their own artistic gifts, a DJ who had actually succeeded on that kind of talent was a charismatic figure, a guru, something like the priest presiding over a rite.

So Y's shift from the dream of shooting a film to the dream of becoming a DJ was probably the natural course of things.

## The Negotiation

He was an industry man, a good talker, skilled at every kind of negotiation.

He had every confidence in that talent himself and believed there was nothing he could not do, and he did, in fact, have an unreal ability to make things happen.

After a few months of practicing as a DJ, he began to dream of throwing a party of his own.

Around then he met the owner of Nine Bar, got close to him, and became the kind of friend you pass a chillum around with.

With every piece of the situation set up in advance, he went in to negotiate, and came back with the right to DJ at Nine Bar.

For a Goa trance DJ, playing Nine Bar was the goal and the dream.

Back in your own country, saying you had DJed at Nine Bar carried real weight, and more DJ work came in.

That was exactly why the DJ calendar there was full far into the future, and why it had become the kind of place where experienced DJs from all over the world were glad to play for free.

For a DJ out of nowhere with six months of experience to play a place like that was a dream beyond a dream, but making that kind of impossible thing happen was exactly what Y did.

He got something close to rapture out of hitting goals that everyone thought were out of reach.

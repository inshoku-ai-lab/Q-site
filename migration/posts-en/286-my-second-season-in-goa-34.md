## More on Top

After taking sixteen times the normal dose of acid, I went back to the dance floor and moved to the music, but it still wasn't clicking.

Normally, too much LSD locks your body up, but that wasn't it, and the acid was definitely working.

My vision was bending, the sound was bending, and my mind was wide awake.

Sixteen times the normal dose was already in me, and it still felt like not enough; greedy as I was, I decided to add another half tab of Hofmann on top.

All together, that worked out to twenty-four times the normal dose.

## The Reason

Why so much acid still hadn't sent me flying was something I would only figure out about a month later.

The cause was the DMT I had taken the day before.

The DMT had thrown me into another dimension and used up every last bit of adrenaline in my brain, so taking acid brought no rush of adrenaline with it, and it felt to me like I wasn't flying.

But flying or not, the LSD was working.

The amount of LSD in my blood was insane, and there was still plenty of DMT in there on top of it.

Acid at twenty-four times the normal strength called up the DMT still in my blood, the two reacted with each other, and what came out of it was a hallucinatory state on a scale beyond any other dimension.

## Boost

This third dose of acid was the boost that smashed through the wall of consciousness.

My vision started to bend hard, and the music echoed back and forth inside my brain.

In the middle of a party, in a state like that, the chillum still comes around to you. That is Goa culture.

But this time, for the first time since I had started going to parties at all, I turned the chillum down in the middle of a party.

I had already hit my limit, and my consciousness was on the verge of collapse.

By then I could barely walk, but I gave the dance floor a try anyway.

I was well past the level where dancing or not dancing to the music was even a question, and all I could do was stand there stock-still in the middle of the dance floor.

I could just barely register that I was at a party, but I couldn't take in anything going on around me.

Then again, a lot of the other people at that party were in much the same state.

Everyone had taken huge amounts of LSD, out to blow the last party of the season wide open.

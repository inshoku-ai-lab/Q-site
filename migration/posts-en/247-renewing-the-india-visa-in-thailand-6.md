## South Africa

Everyone was making preparations for a destination of their own, and South Africa drew the most attention of any of them.

The reason was that a group of party organizers in Cape Town was putting on the biggest millennium party in the world, with top artists from every country lined up to DJ there for New Year's 2000.

But for the travelers who lived for psytrance, it was not so much which DJs would be playing that mattered; what mattered was whether they would be there at the biggest celebration on earth.

For travelers — above all the ones who lived as travelers, who had staked their lives on the road — where you rang in the year 2000 mattered more than anything.

Celebrating the turn of a millennium is, obviously enough, something that comes around only once in a thousand years, and the mood in the air was that if you had been born into this particular era, spending that moment under the best conditions you could find was simply what a traveler did.

## Competition

Among those travelers, the ones with a strong masculine streak were itching to compete with everyone else, full of the drive to have a better time than anybody on the planet.

I thought much the same way myself, and so did a lot of my friends, each of us charging ahead with whatever choice we believed was the best one.

Sometimes it turned into an argument over where the best place to spend New Year's Eve was, and you would catch people sneering a little at anyone who had chosen differently.

## Goa

Out of all the options, I chose Goa.

The reason was that Goa the year before had hit me so hard that I could not imagine getting started on anything else in my life until I had seen what the next year's Goa would be.

Beyond that, two other things mattered: helping my close friend Y with his film shoot, and the reunion in India with I-chan, my girlfriend, who I had left behind in Japan and who was going to appear in it.

Part of me was drawn to the South Africa option, but it felt like I was not ready to make that journey yet.

Whether or not the party there would be any good, I did not have enough experience on the road, or enough nerve, to travel across Africa.

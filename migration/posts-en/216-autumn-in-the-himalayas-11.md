## Organized by Tibetans

This time the party was put on by young Tibetans.

The son of the restaurant we always ate at, along with his crew of rowdy troublemakers, had worked something out with a hotel and put on the party.

We took auto-rickshaws over to the village of Old Manali, on the other side of the valley, paid the entrance fee at the hotel, and joined the party.

After going to this many parties I had naturally gotten better at preparing, and I could put together whatever I was going to need ahead of time.

What I brought was a pile of joints rolled from high-quality cannabis, packed into an empty cigarette box until it was full.

When LSD hits too hard, it tenses you up, mind and body both, which makes the loosening effect of cannabis very important.

Psytrance music itself is also composed to deliver its maximum effect when you take LSD and cannabis at the same time, so out of respect for the artists it was important to get as wrecked as possible and keep dancing.

Once the LSD has you that far gone, you lose the fine control of your fingers, and rolling a joint becomes extremely difficult.

Hence the "boxed lunch."

## The Party

This was a party thrown by rowdy kids who had grown up among hippie travelers, so they knew exactly what they were doing, and it turned out to be a good one.

All of us dropped LSD together, got wrecked, and kept dancing.

Full-out and feeling good, we danced right through to morning.

The morning light slanting into the hotel courtyard lit us up, and the explosion of unhinged grins on the faces of friends I knew so well lifted anyone watching them into an even greater happiness.

The night was over now, and as the party moved toward its end, we spent the last of that good time dancing together.

## Something's Wrong

But then, all at once, the organizers broke into an uproar.

At that point I still hadn't noticed and was dancing like a lunatic with a stupid grin on my face, but Y — a former bōsōzoku and a fighter — caught the danger right away and came running over to me.

Y said something strange was going on, and the police might be here.

The chemical drugs — the LSD and the rest — I had already taken, so none of that was on me, but the cigarette box was still packed with all those joints, so just to be safe I took it out of my bag and hid it in the grass nearby.

At that moment the Tibetan party organizers came flying out of the hotel and bolted straight into the forest.

## Departure Day

At last the day of departure arrived.

Our flight wasn't leaving from Heathrow, the usual choice, but from Luton Airport, a minor one.

Departure was at five in the morning, so we spent the night before at the airport, ready for takeoff.

## A Budget Airline

When we got to the airport, the check-in counter turned out to be at the very far end, in the most inconvenient spot there was.

The reason for all this was that we had chosen easyJet, an ultra-cheap airline.

The company apparently managed to sell tickets at that price by cutting costs on every front.

Normally you picture the people working for an airline as beautiful, cultured-looking women, but the woman who checked us in at this counter was a rough-looking woman of the yankee type.

You could tell at a glance this was not your ordinary airline.

It's common now, but back then this was one of the very first paperless tickets: you had to print it out yourself, and what they handed you once you were inside the airport wasn't a paper ticket but a plastic card that looked like it got used over and over.

Every last detail had gone into getting the price down.

On board, the seats were absurdly narrow, so tight that my knees knocked against the seat in front.

Normally food and drinks on a plane come free, but on easyJet you had to pay for them, and one small thing after another kept surprising me.

When it came to safety, though, they did things properly, and the plane landed in Madrid without the slightest problem.

## Madrid

We got off the plane and set foot on Spanish soil.

Flying is fun, but standing on the ground suits a human being better.

I had already looked up how to get from the airport to the long-distance bus station, so everything went smoothly.

The Madrid train network was color-coded in a way that was easy to follow, simple enough for someone who couldn't speak Spanish to understand.

It must be a city with a lot of international traffic.

After a short wait at the bus stop, we boarded an overnight bus bound for Algeciras, at the southernmost tip of Spain.

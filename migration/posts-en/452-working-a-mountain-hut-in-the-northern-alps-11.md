## Eccentricities

With the trail improved and the mountain now promoted as an easy climb for beginners, older hikers with little mountain experience began arriving in droves.

At the same time, though, accidents on the mountain started to increase.

And strangely enough, most of those accidents kept happening in one particular spot.

In one case, a climber developed heart and lung trouble serious enough to need a helicopter rescue.

Others had heart problems, or trouble with their knees and ankles.

Accidents involving climbers are the last thing anyone running a mountain hut wants.

## The Medium

If accidents kept happening in the same area, the president reasoned, then some evil earthbound spirit had to be lurking around there, so he decided to call in a medium to perform an exorcism.

Through his network of fellow believers in "the god of management," he knew all sorts of psychics.

A few weeks after the accidents started piling up, the medium arrived.

The medium was coming on business, not as a climber.

A great master who warranted special treatment, the medium was flown in by helicopter.

## Leopard Print

When the medium stepped off the helicopter, the staff were floored.

Many of them said they could barely keep from laughing.

The medium was a heavily made-up middle-aged woman in black tights and a leopard-print bodycon dress.

The president welcomed the honored medium with great courtesy, gave her the best room in the hut, and waited on her attentively.

The next day, the medium went down the mountain and performed the exorcism in that area where the bad energy was said to hang in the air.

The ritual apparently consisted mostly of shrieking and shouting in strange voices, and the staff were all so stunned they had no idea how to react.

In the end, after about an hour of solemn shrieking, she went on down the mountain.

All told, the ritual reportedly cost several hundred thousand yen, a few thousand dollars.

## Countermeasures

The person who took issue with the medium's ritual was M at the cable-car relay hut.

According to him, accidents kept happening at that spot because there weren't enough benches.

If there were benches at reasonable intervals along the trail, tired climbers would stop and rest on them, and there would be no more accidents from people pushing on through their exhaustion.

Convinced he was right, M built new benches right after the ritual.

Whether it was the ritual or the new benches, there were apparently no more accidents at that spot after that.

All that was left was the legend of the strange medium.

# The Next Stage

From this article onward, I will be publishing in two versions: a members-only edition and a complete edition (free).

There are several reasons for this, and since the occasion calls for it, let me explain from the very beginning of these Vagabond Chronicles.

# How It Started

There are three main reasons why I began writing these chronicles.

- What I have gone through in my life is far too exceptional an experience, not the sort of thing just anyone can live through.

- Simply knowing that a life experience like this exists has the power to liberate people's consciousness.

- I want people to awaken to the power of the free will they have possessed all along.

The last one in particular — "I want people to awaken to the power of the free will they have possessed all along" — is a very strong desire, and everything I do converges on that point.

Partly because I did in fact grow up in an extremely repressive environment myself, I sincerely wish for every person to awaken to freedom.

# Travel and Writing

The way many travelers live is filled with inspiration, and there is much in it that has a profound effect on others.

But people who travel in earnest, staking their lives on it, do not spend all day sitting at a computer, patiently putting sentences together.

And even when they do write, not everyone has a gift for it, so their writing is rarely read or passed around.

Conversely, a person with a gift for writing who can sit at a computer all day is almost never someone taking the kind of interesting, life-or-death journey that a whole life is staked on.

An interesting travelogue and an interesting travel experience are two different things.

# What Sets Me Apart

Holding these opposites together is, as I understand it, what most sets me apart.

The introverted, nerdy temperament I have by nature, and the outrageous travel experiences that broke straight through it.

Having wandered the world to exhaustion, I find the urge to travel now spent, and can concentrate on writing.

And a knack for putting what I think into words (until recently, I did not know I had any gift for writing).

# A Bare Record

To convey these things accurately, I express myself through the form of a vagabond chronicle.

And to get it across in the best possible way, I believe it is important to lay everything bare on the page.

But a bare record sometimes includes a great deal that is far too private, or that one hesitates to speak of in front of others.

When I first started writing these chronicles, I intended to make everything public exactly as it was, but weighing various factors, I concluded that making it all public was not the wisest course.

# Going Forward

So from here on, the articles will be split into a complete edition (free) and a members-only edition.

In the complete edition I will set down the entire record of my life, bare and exactly as it was.

There is nothing halfhearted about the way of life of a human being who threw everything away and devoted his life to travel.

Even through the indirect form of a vagabond chronicle, simply knowing that a human being living this way actually exists has great power to liberate the minds and hearts of those who read it.

Please take this opportunity to come one step closer and catch a glimpse of the unknown world of travelers.

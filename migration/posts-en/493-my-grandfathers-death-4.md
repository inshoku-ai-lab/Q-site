## Cremation

After the funeral, he was taken to the local crematorium to be cremated.

A few hours of burning later, all that was left of my grandfather was a pile of ashes small enough to scoop up in two hands.

A human life is a fleeting thing.

Every time I came back from India, my grandfather used to say he'd like to go to India himself someday.

Looking at those ashes, I decided to take his remains to India.

If I'd taken him there alive, he'd probably have been dead within a week.

But now that he was already dead and reduced to ashes, I could pack him in my backpack and bring him along.

The next time I went to India, I would let him go into the Ganges, the same way India's holy men do.

With that resolution firmly made, I finished saying goodbye to my grandfather.

## My Grandfather's Secret

My family was cursed with gambling and debt, and my grandfather was no exception.

In his case, pachinko was his whole life.

Once every two months, when his pension came in, he'd be lined up outside the pachinko parlor first thing in the morning, and within a few weeks the whole thing was gone.

He killed the days left until the next payment doing jigsaw puzzles. That was how he spent his old age.

Everyone in the family saw his pachinko habit as a problem, but we took comfort in the fact that at least he wasn't borrowing money.

We were wrong.

It came out only after he died: for decades, right up to the end, he had been carrying a debt the whole time.

The amount always hovered around 300,000 yen, about twenty-four hundred dollars, and apparently he kept it going like clockwork, borrowing and paying back, paying back and borrowing again.

Part of the reason was simply that he was sloppy, but there was one more reason besides that.

As it turned out, he'd taken a liking to a middle-aged woman who worked in the office of the consumer loan company.

Apparently he'd kept dragging the debt out so he could see her once every two months.

It's a dark story, but there's something heartwarming in it all the same.

It only came to light because my grandfather died.

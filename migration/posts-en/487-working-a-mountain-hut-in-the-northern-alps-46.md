## The End of the Conversation

Having said what she had to say, I-chan walked away, as if a weight had come off her.

She seemed to have been torn between not wanting to hurt me, not wanting to lie to me, and wanting K to like her.

We headed for the banquet hall, walking a little apart.

All kinds of thoughts ran through my head, and in the confusion I couldn't feel the ground under my feet.

## The Banquet Hall

The banquet hall, meanwhile, was full of noise and cheer.

The season's work at the huts was over, safely and completely, without a single death or injury, and the company had turned a big profit, so the president couldn't have been happier.

The party started with a toast led by the president.

Everyone poured drinks for one another and thanked each other for everything, day after day.

It was my first time taking part in a traditional Japanese custom like this, so it surprised me a little, but once I tried it, it felt good, and pouring someone a drink turned out to be a warm way to show my gratitude.

## In High Spirits

Everything had gone his way, so naturally the president was in high spirits.

We employees had been exploited pretty badly, but we still had real money left in our hands, and now that it was all over, everyone was all smiles.

There wasn't a trace of stress anywhere.

At a mountain hut there's no rent, no food bill, not even anywhere to spend money, so no matter how rough the work gets, you can put real savings away, and that's something to be thankful for.

Especially for someone like me, with no education, no connections, no credentials, and no experience, a chance to save hundreds of thousands of yen, a few thousand dollars, in just a few months of work doesn't come along often.

On top of that, you work out in nature alongside good people, and by the time the job is done, you're all muscle.

A traveler who wants to get back on the road has to quit after a few months of work.

That's never easy to bring up, and you feel bad about it, but at a mountain hut everyone's work ends in November, so it isn't a problem at all.

## Gandalf

Out in a desert with nothing in it, it was surprising enough that there was "something" there at all; more surprising that the something "moved"; and more surprising still when it turned out to be a "human being."

To top it off, the human being was Gandalf himself.

He must have been just as surprised as we were, though.

He can't have expected a young Asian couple to come walking out of the middle of the desert weeks before the festival even started.

We stood there staring each other up and down, astonished and a little awed.

Warily, I asked him in English, "Do you speak English?"

To my surprise, Gandalf answered gently, in fluent English: "Ah, yes, I speak English. Hello."

As far as I was concerned he had been a completely unknown object, but apparently he was a person, personality and all.

From the look of him, a Westerner.

## Who He Was

Once we got talking he turned out to be a nice man, and he told us all sorts of things.

That he was the one who had started the festival.

That a friend of his was organizing the main dance floor, while he was in charge of organizing the chill-out area.

He had come out today, it seemed, to give the site a final check.

When we told him we had fallen for the flyer at first sight and come all the way from London, he was delighted.

## Dinner

The people putting the festival together had already started to gather, and apparently they were all living together in a big house rented for the purpose.

In an hour or so a friend was coming to pick him up and they were all going to eat together, he said — would we like to come along?

Getting to know an eccentric old wizard like this was about the highest honor we could imagine, so we said yes on the spot and joined them for dinner.

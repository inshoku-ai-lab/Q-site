## Home Again

That day, once dinner was over, they drove us back to our hotel.

Even back in our room, we were too keyed up about the day's unexpected turn to get to sleep for a long time.

Up until that morning we had been living the life of the Sahara's people to the fullest, and then we met an old wizard, and one thing led straight to the next: we were carried off to a Western-style mansion, met all sorts of people, were treated to a Western-style feast, and came home.

A lot of it had been interesting, but the main thing was the question of who on earth that old man was.

From the look of him, he was without a doubt a seasoned hippie.

I-chan's guess, going by his English accent, was that he was British.

But no matter how you looked at him, he didn't seem like someone who lived in Britain.

He fit into Morocco far too well.

His skin was tanned dark, and the way the djellaba, the Moroccan robe, suited him, and the sheer presence he gave off, were something out of the ordinary.

Did he live in Ouarzazate?

We had fallen under Gandalf's strange spell.

## A Few Days Later

One day, a few days after that first meeting, Gandalf turned up at our hotel.

Dressed like a wizard, as usual.

He seemed to speak French, and came up to our room chatting with the manager.

There was a party tonight, he said, so he had come by to invite us while he was out shopping.

The staff had apparently split up to run errands, and were going to gather here once the shopping was done.

We hurried through our preparations and got ready to go out.

## The Party

While we waited at a café outside the hotel, the team members who had finished their shopping arrived one after another.

We met them one by one and introduced ourselves.

Every one of them had a strong personality and was brimming with energy, and there was an air about them of being interesting people.

After a while a car pulled up, and we all set off for the mansion in the desert.

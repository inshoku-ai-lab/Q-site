## Stories Around the Campfire 2

There were all kinds of stories, but the most shocking one was Gandalf's.

He was getting divorced, of all things. It had been decided just before the festival started.

He had mentioned a wife and daughters, but it seems he'd never gone into detail because there was a complicated story behind it.

He didn't share the details, but I-chan and I, guessing on our own, imagined he had thrown himself into his personal mission of waking people up to ecology and saving the planet, and hadn't paid much attention to his family.

We never knew what really happened, but seeing that even a man of Gandalf's character could fail at love brought home to us, once again, how hard life can be.

At the same time, the image of perfection we'd pinned on the old wizard fell apart, and we began to see Gandalf as just a human being living his own life.

## His Age

Even more shocking was finding out how old Gandalf was.

He had been in charge of organizing the chill-out area, and with his presence and his big heart, he led people brilliantly and carried the festival to success.

Between looking exactly like an old wizard and having more life experience than seemed possible, we'd assumed he was a very old hippie.

Somewhere between sixty-five and seventy-five, probably.

But his daughters, he said, were eight and eleven.

For the age we'd guessed, the kids were far too young.

That was when someone asked him how old he was.

Nobody could hide their surprise at the answer, but the gap was so huge that nobody could say a thing.

For all the way he looked, he was forty-eight.

We'd taken him for an old man who had wandered the world and settled into retirement, and here he was, a father in his prime with kids in the early grades of elementary school.

Freshly divorced, too.

We had made up the image ourselves and believed it ourselves, so we had only ourselves to blame, but what a surprise it was.

He had the air of a battle-hardened warlord or military strategist from Japan's Edo period, when a human life was said to last fifty years.

You never can tell with people.

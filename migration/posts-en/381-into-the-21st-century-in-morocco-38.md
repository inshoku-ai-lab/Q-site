## Flipping Out

After the sunset, on our way to the big Sahara-style tent where our friends had gathered, we heard a rumor.

A-kun, who had been helping Gandalf, had taken too much LSD and flipped out.

To flip out is to have an experience so intense that your normal consciousness drops away, and something like the subconscious takes over.

Other ways to put it: he snapped, he lost it, he's gone, he's possessed, he isn't coming back.

## A-kun

A-kun, twenty years old, was the son of a Moroccan hippie couple who were friends of Gandalf's, and they had left him in Gandalf's care for this festival so he could get some experience of the working world.

That was how he came to be at Gandalf's side, working hard to help with the festival.

Helping out on an organizing team that ran on nothing but stress must have put him under a great deal of stress himself.

Now that they had finally managed to get the festival open, maybe he felt he could relax at last.

## A Breather

It was probably a chance for the whole festival crew to catch their breath, too.

D shared his prized Malana cream, and he shared his special Swiss LSD.

Of course, D and the others and Gandalf all took it too.

What made this different from the LSD at the Christmas party the other night was that this time we were at a festival, a lawless zone.

D and Gandalf alike showed their true colors as seasoned hippies and took huge amounts of LSD.

Every job was done. All that was left was to leave the rest to fate and get wrecked.

The rocket had already launched.

## The End

In the end, Y never went back to the decks — time just ran on, and his big night at Nine Bar was over.

He seemed to know it himself — "I blew it" — but there was nothing to be done.

So the night ended in a deflated mood, and we all headed home.

He himself knew how badly he had failed, how ridiculous a thing he had done, and that was exactly what left no way to console him.

He had taken the biggest chance he had ever created for himself and wrecked it in the most ridiculous way possible.

There was no way he wasn't kicking himself.

My girlfriend and I, and our other friends too, had planned to close out the season with this party and then head off, each of us to wherever we were going next.

That was exactly why it was meant to be the last goodbye party, and it ended without ever really taking off.

## Plans

I had been turning over all kinds of ideas about what came next.

This time in Goa I had made my money selling charas and LSD hauled down from the Himalayas, but I didn't want to keep running that kind of risky business forever.

Keep at it long enough and sooner or later you get caught.

Rather than making money the easy way, I wanted to work all kinds of jobs and pile up life experience — that was how I saw it.

When I thought about where abroad I could earn a decent living and actually get by, Europe was where I naturally landed.

Three countries interested me.

One was Germany.

The reason was that I loved the German music scene.

Seventies German rock and krautrock, nineties German trance, minimal techno, house — a lot of the music I was really into had been born in the German underground scene.

Since I meant to spend my life as a musician, I wanted to experience that scene at its core, in the place it came from.

Another country that interested me was the Netherlands.

The Netherlands was famous for legalized cannabis — you could smoke as freely as you liked, with nothing to worry about.

I wanted to experience the free air of Europe for myself.

I had also heard a rumor that the people who came to Goa regularly gathered there and threw parties, so I figured I might get an interesting experience out of it.

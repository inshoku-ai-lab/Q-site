## Ecology

I learned an enormous amount from this communal life.

First, and more than anything else, it changed my awareness of ecology.

This was 2001, back when almost no one in Japan was talking about ecology.

I knew the word and the idea, but I had never once given it any thought.

A twenty-two-year-old who had grown up in the city wasn't mature enough to think about the future of the entire planet.

If anything, what I had learned in India was not to care about tossing trash in the street.

In India nobody cared, and the endless trash along the roadsides had become part of everyday life.

Instead of learning how to fix that, I had learned how not to care about it.

## Mission

Gandalf worked to raise awareness about ecology, so that the future of this planet could one day be entrusted to selfish, self-centered young people like me.

Organizing the festival had been part of that effort, and so was this communal life.

He had devoted his life to that mission, sacrificing everything for the sake of this planet.

His tireless work was paying off little by little, and I think a good many people were inspired by it to change the way they saw things.

Of all of them, I think I was one of the people most influenced by his ecological thinking.

For the rest of my life, coexisting with the Earth would be one of its central themes.

What really matters to the Earth?

What does it mean to live alongside the Earth?

From then on, my life would unfold from what Gandalf taught me.

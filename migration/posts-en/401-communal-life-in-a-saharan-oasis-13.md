## Visa

M's momentum was focused entirely on the moment, the "here and now," and all of it went into living each day to the fullest.

The flip side was that he ignored anything resembling a long-term plan.

In his case, that showed up in the form of an expiring visa.

A Moroccan visa is good for three months, which is plenty for most travelers, but for M it wasn't enough.

His visa had already been about to run out when he got to the oasis, yet he showed no sign at all of doing anything about it, and before long it had expired.

To M, it was a "So?..." kind of thing; it didn't even seem to register as a problem.

Since he wasn't worried about it, it wasn't for anyone else to worry about, and we trusted M's flow and left it to him.

Once his visa ran out, he seemed to have let go of even more, and M shone freer and brighter than ever.

## Full Moon

We had been living in the desert for a while when a full moon night came around.

Gandalf, it turned out, had been getting ready well in advance to throw a party for it.

I only heard about it on the day, but I'll take any party, anytime.

Right at that moment, D and E showed up, two seasoned traveling musicians.

I-chan and I were both huge fans of theirs, so their surprise visit thrilled us.

We were happy, but D's aura was so different from ours that it was hard to just relax and get friendly with him.

Still, like a lovestruck girl happy just to be near them, we were glad to spend the full moon in their company.

# Bangkok

Coming into Bangkok, I noticed the dirty sky and the hurry everyone was in.

Unlike the jungle-and-beach days behind me, the streets were overflowing with shops.

Nature on its own is good, but the convenience and clutter of a city aren't bad either.

There were barbershops too, so I decided to get my head cleaned up.

Back on Koh Phangan I had tried to shave my head and botched it, but the cuts and the sunburn had mostly healed, so this seemed like about the right time.

The barber, a young guy, took one look at my ridiculous haircut and his eyes went wide.

In a Buddhist country he must have been used to shaved heads, though.

As soon as I got to Bangkok, I bought a bus ticket for northern Thailand.

The plan was not to spend too long in the big city — see the mountain countryside of Thailand first, and save the last days for Bangkok.

# Thai Massage

With time on my hands, I went sightseeing here and there with a guidebook.

A few minutes' walk from Khao San Road, the travelers' hub where I was staying, there was apparently a temple where you could get traditional Thai massage, so I decided to go.

At this temple you could apparently also learn massage.

In Japan I was bad at going somewhere I didn't know, or walking into a building I couldn't make sense of — it took real nerve — but coming to Thailand as a traveler, I could step into unfamiliar places fairly easily.

A lot of that, I think, was the easygoing air Thai people have and their loose, take-it-as-it-comes way of doing things, which made everything feel approachable.

Living with my heart open like that was a first for me, and it felt incredibly fresh and good.

At the temple I said I wanted a massage, and was shown into a room with futon mattresses laid out on the floor.

The air conditioning was going.

After the street outside, steaming with heat thrown back off the asphalt, it felt better still.

I changed into the shirt and pants they had ready, took off the towel covering my freshly shaved head, and lay down.

The monk who would be giving the massage, shaven-headed himself, saw my freshly shaved head and lit up.

Did he think I was a Buddhist too?

If anything, I was going for punk.

Thai massage felt so good it softened even me, a skinhead punk with a ravaged heart.

It was the first time my body had felt good in a way that softened me, and it surprised me that such a feeling existed.

# The Strip Club

Around then I got friendly with a Japanese guy staying at the guesthouse.

Talking to someone easily and becoming friends was unthinkable back in Japan, so this too came as a surprise, but that must be the magic of Thailand.

He was a blond, well-built student at the University of Tokyo — the top school in the country — and a self-proclaimed strip club researcher.

He said he'd teach me how to enjoy a strip club properly.

I liked the sleazy, chaotic feel of Nishinari, so seeing a strip club interested me too.

I had heard that Thailand was full of sex joints, and that you could actually have sex there, and at eighteen I was intensely curious.

Maybe because of how twisted my upbringing was, I carried a kind of fear about romance with girls.

There had been chances enough during my part-time-job life in Osaka, but shyness always won and nothing ever came of it.

I had been thinking that if the chance came up in Thailand I would like to lose my virginity, but I didn't have the nerve to walk into a place like that alone, so his invitation was a stroke of luck.

Everybody, I suppose, wants their first time to be with a girl they love, but I didn't believe I'd manage an ordinary relationship any time soon, so that idea was never even on the table for me.

My new friend took me to the red-light district called Patpong Street.

Hearing "Thai red-light district," I had pictured something run-down on the edge of town, but the real thing turned out to be as glittering as Las Vegas.

Neon blazing, young women selling their beauty. The touts were relentless.

My strip club researcher friend knew exactly how to operate, and made straight for the place he had in mind.

Inside, beautiful young Thai women were dancing, throwing inviting looks.

It was the first time I had seen a naked woman in real life — a shock, and it left me tense.

We sat down and ordered beer.

Drinking beer in a bar was another first, and so was being drunk.

The last time had been in grade school, tasting my uncle's beer and crying at how foul it was.

My friend had already found the girl he wanted and started getting cozy with her.

Watching people get cozy right in front of me was another first.

I was tense and flustered.

They were talking about something in English, but I couldn't follow it.

They were probably negotiating the price of the sex that would follow.

He came over and told me the deal was done, and they were off to a room.

When I told him I didn't really understand what was going on and was lost, he said this place wasn't right for me and that I should go to a building a few minutes' walk away.

A strip club researcher indeed — there seemed to be no situation he couldn't handle.

# The Brothel

Where I was headed was not a strip club but a brothel.

Strip clubs, apparently, suit people who speak English and want to enjoy the conversation and the haggling; people who don't understand English are better off going straight to a brothel.

What was waiting there was a building far bigger than I had imagined.

My imagination had let me down again.

What went on there was against the law and against Buddhist morality, but apparently Thai easygoingness let it pass.

I handed over money at the reception desk, went inside, and there on a tiered platform stood some fifty women, lined up and beaming out in my direction.

It was quite a spectacle.

The attendant told me to pick whichever girl I liked.

Out of all of them I chose a woman who gave off a grown-up kind of appeal.

The sort who looked like she would guide a virgin through it gently.

Unlike the strip club, the brothel moved overwhelmingly fast.

Briskly shown to a room, briskly undressed, taken to the shower and washed.

Before I knew it I was laid out on the bed.

A veteran prostitute, sure enough — things moved fast.

This isn't a porn blog, so I'll skip the details from here, but my first time was over almost before it began, and strictly as a matter of business.

# Obsession

Looking back now, it was a pretty shabby way to lose my virginity, but at the time I was perfectly satisfied.

I had never touched a woman outside my family, and since early childhood I had barely been touched by anyone at all, so the contact of skin on skin was itself a shock like electricity going through me, and it felt as though something in me had been let loose.

I think I carried a kind of obsession about the word "virgin."

Maybe it was brainwashing by the media, but I believed the younger you were when you did it the cooler you were, and that eighteen was already late.

The inferiority I felt about having been born into a Jehovah's Witness household was strong too, I think.

I wanted to experience something ahead of my classmates from high school.

Knowing that while everyone else was still sitting in school studying, I had already lost my virginity, gave my sense of superiority an enormous jolt.

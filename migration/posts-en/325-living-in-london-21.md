## D's Preparations

Before coming to England, D had been in Thailand.

Apparently he had been taking it easy on the southern islands and in Bangkok.

Then it was settled that he and T would go to Glastonbury Festival, and he started getting ready.

There were the flights and the festival tickets to arrange, but he also needed the gear to survive a festival that runs for several days.

Thailand is the tropics, so you can carry nothing and be fine, but in England, summer or not, if the rain comes while you are out in the open you will be freezing by the middle of the night.

Not just rain gear, of course, but a tent and the rest of it.

On top of that, hoping to cover his travel costs and his spending money, he had a big batch of T-shirts made in Thailand to sell at the festival.

In any case, to make a dream he had held for years come true, he had prepared flawlessly.

## The Road to London

D flew out of Thailand on Biman Bangladesh Airlines, a budget carrier, headed for London by way of Dhaka in Bangladesh.

Back then, cheap flights around India meant Biman Air, and the airline was famous enough that anyone traveling in India knew the name.

It was talked about not just for the cheap fares but for the bad quality.

Flights ran late, the service was bad, or so they said.

But that is exactly the kind of plane used by travelers who only want to reach their destination cheap.

## The Mysterious Delay

We were excited, waiting for D to arrive, when the day before he was due in London a sudden email came saying he might not make it on time.

Astonishingly, he had lost his passport on board the Biman plane.

There was no time to explain now, he would be in touch with the details later, and there the email cut off.

The email did surprise us, but Biman Air running late was par for the course, and there were still days to go before the festival, so we figured it was nothing to worry about and settled in to wait.

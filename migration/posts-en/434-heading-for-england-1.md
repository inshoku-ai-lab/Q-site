## Goodbye

I-chan's flight left before mine, so I saw her off to Japan.

It was a parting we had both agreed to, so there was no sadness in it.

We had come to terms with it together, and we could send each other off with a smile.

I had no idea what would happen from here, but I knew the day would come when we'd meet again.

The feeling was overwhelming, but it wasn't a bad one.

I held on to it as I headed for London.

A new life was waiting for me there.

## England

The plane swam through the sky for about an hour, and in no time I was in a foreign land.

As planned, I went straight to N's place.

I was going to lean on her hospitality for a while again.

It's a blessing to have a best friend from the road who really understands you.

I could always count on her, and we'd help each other out with anything.

My plan was to find somewhere new to live during the few days I stayed.

It was the first time we'd seen each other in four months, but N hadn't really changed.

Her skin had gotten paler, maybe, but that was about it.

Next to her, I was tanned almost black.

What I'd been through in those four months was beyond measure.

## At N's Place

To celebrate seeing each other again after so long, we ate a good meal and talked.

From here on, I wanted to live in London and throw myself into making music.

Most of it would be composing on a computer, so I'd already had my computer shipped over from Japan.

My mother suggested that with shipping so expensive, maybe I'd be better off buying a new one in London, but I overruled her and had it sent anyway.

My savings had run dry, so buying a new computer right away was out of the question, but I wanted to get back to making music immediately.

With I-chan throwing herself into theater training in Japan, I wanted to use this chance to devote myself to art as well.

## Morning

We were riding high after catching the first sunrise of the year with such incredible timing.

At this rate, 2002, which was just beginning, was bound to be a wonderful year.

It was the kind of sunrise that stirs up a fantasy: that even when things look hard, they sort themselves out at the crucial moment and everything goes right...

The climbers who had come up to greet the first sunrise headed back down, and once again it was just the two of us.

We'd come all the way to the summit, after all, and we wanted to take our time.

## A Dream

Then H said something.

"I want to do Bom."

Bom is the Hindi word for the ritual of smoking cannabis, so "I want to do Bom" meant he wanted to smoke some cannabis.

I had traveled India myself, smoking cannabis all the time, so I knew exactly how he felt.

A moment this perfect, right after a perfect sunrise, was exactly when you'd want to do Bom and give thanks to the universe.

I agreed with H wholeheartedly, but at the same time I knew full well that no such miracle could happen.

The summit of a mountain on an island at the far edge of Japan, a country that comes down hard on cannabis.

Forget cannabis — we weren't even going to run into another person.

## The Next Climbers

Ten minutes after H said "I want to do Bom," someone came up to the summit.

Looking down from the top, we could see three young people.

One was a guy with dreadlocks, like Bob Marley.

Another was a girl in loud, colorful clothes.

The third was a smiling, likable guy.

They had the same hippie-traveler air about them that we did.

We recognized each other as our own kind, said hello with big smiles, and started talking.

It turned out that Y, the one with the dreadlocks, had traveled in India before and had moved to Ishigaki Island about two years earlier.

The other two had come to visit him.

They had been heading for the summit to see the first sunrise but had fumbled one thing after another and missed it, and since they'd made it to the foot of the mountain anyway, they climbed on up to the top.

## The Next Miracle

The moment Y mentioned India, H jumped right in: "Got any Bom?"

Y answered as if it were the most natural thing in the world: "Yeah, I've got some. Want to smoke?"

At that, H and I went wild, practically dancing for joy.

Neither of us had imagined for a second that H's one little wish would actually come true, so the miracle stunned us.

Something we'd wished for just ten minutes earlier had instantly become real and appeared right in front of us.

Things that happy don't happen often.

We thanked Lord Shiva, thanked the workings of the universe and its miracles, and did Bom to celebrate the first sunrise of 2002.

Every one of us was certain this was going to be the best year ever.

## Character

I met a lot of people with strong personalities on this trip to Morocco, and C was right up there with them.

When most people picture someone from the countryside, they probably think of words like wild, rough, simple, kind, good-natured, taciturn, warmhearted, and emotional.

C embodied that image perfectly, with the single exception of "taciturn."

He was a French ranch hand who took pride in living simply and living tough.

That simplicity and roughness sometimes ran out of control, and it showed most clearly whenever we were doing something together as a group.

## Jam Sessions

When we all had a jam session (improvising together on instruments), for example, he'd go off on his own, drifting completely out of the rhythm and pounding away furiously on a drum by himself.

His view was that the most important thing was for the person playing to enjoy it, and that if everyone enjoyed themselves in their own way, everything would naturally work out.

In a sense it was a free-jazz way of thinking, and not exactly "wrong," but more often than not, his drum drowned out everyone else's playing, and he ended up the only one left.

At times like that, Gandalf, in his role as the elder, would gently reason with C, and C would get a lesson in matching the rhythm.

That kind of thing didn't seem to suit C, though, and playing together in time with everyone else was something he never managed, right to the end.

Maybe it was partly his age, and C's personality had already set for good.

## M

Young M, on the other hand, showed an adaptability that was remarkable to see. (Young, though he was still several years older than I was at the time.)

Everyone went through all kinds of changes during this communal life, but M's change was the one I enjoyed watching most.

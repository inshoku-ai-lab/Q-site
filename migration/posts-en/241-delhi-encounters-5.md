## The House in Tokyo

J had a hideout in Tokyo, and he told us to feel free to drop by any time we were back in Japan.

Travelers were always gathering there, he said, having a good time.

And there was always cannabis on hand, so we would have nothing to worry about once we were home.

Looking forward to seeing him again the next chance we got, we said goodbye to J and headed for Thailand.

## What Came Later

A few years after this, J died of a heroin overdose.

It was too early a death, in his mid-fifties, but he had burned hot and short, running flat out the whole way, and that life of his was handed down to a lot of people as the spirit of the road.

The news was too sad for me too, but somewhere deep down it made a strange kind of sense, and I accepted it.

The news raced through the traveler world and saddened a lot of people, but every one of them was celebrating the road J had traveled, a life lived all the way through with no regrets.

A few months after his death, I heard, a huge party was thrown deep in the mountains of Japan to celebrate J's journey into the next world.

Veteran hippies, by all accounts, took LSD in quantity, right to the limit, danced themselves wild to psytrance, and celebrated his road in grand style.

## To Thailand

The point of going to Thailand was to get a new India visa and spend another six months there.

It wasn't that we wanted to go to Thailand in particular, but without picking up the visa somewhere we couldn't spend time in the India we loved, so we had no choice.

But of course, if we were going, we were going to get everything out of it.

Which, for us, also meant smuggling cannabis into Thailand.

Time without cannabis was unthinkable to us, and taking on any risk at all, willingly, was what we understood a traveler's resolve to be.

I was carrying a kilo (two pounds) of hash and a hundred tabs of LSD, and taking all of it to Thailand was far too risky, so I left most of it in luggage storage in Delhi.

About a hundred grams (three and a half ounces) went into a health-food container, the way we always did it, ready to smuggle into Thailand.

We repacked our bags from scratch, and with nothing cannabis-related on us, we headed for the airport.

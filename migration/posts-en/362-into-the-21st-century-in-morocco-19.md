## The Party at the Mansion

When we reached the mansion and went inside, there were clearly more people than there had been a few days before.

Apparently, with the festival date getting closer, more and more helpers would keep arriving from now on.

The festival was about two weeks away, so things were about to get busy.

At the last party, everyone had only just arrived and nobody knew anybody, so it had been a little hard to loosen up. This time, the people who'd been staying a while had already settled into an easy friendliness with each other.

At the same time, though, a whole wave of brand-new people had turned up, and there was a stiffness in the air from people who weren't used to the place yet.

Even while all this was going on, more new people kept arriving.

Everyone, us included, hung back at first in front of such a big crowd of strangers.

But the group of German DJs who showed up later was different.

## The DJs

The three of them, young and bright-faced, walked straight into the crowd without flinching, smiling at everyone, shaking hands and introducing themselves to every single person.

Anyone could see it was the right thing to do, and hardly anyone actually does it. They did, and goodwill spread outward from them in a widening circle.

Their arrival changed the whole mood at once.

People had been holding back out of politeness, tense and keeping their distance; now it felt as if it was fine to just relax and be friends, and the room suddenly filled with chatter.

After all, everyone here loved the same music, and everyone loved the feel of travel and the hippie vibe.

Once people started opening up, it didn't take long.

## R

Everyone here was a traveler, and everyone loved psytrance, but the feel was very different depending on whether someone was here mainly for the traveling or mainly for the psytrance.

The travelers were soft and earthy; the psytrance crowd was cool and urban.

I'd grown up in Osaka, had come here from London this time, and went to psytrance events all the time, so I thought of myself as more of a psytrance person than a traveler. Apparently I was wrong.

Compared with the people here, I clearly clicked better with the ones who leaned toward traveling.

R, the French artist we'd made friends with at the last party, must have felt the same way, because he seemed to feel a real kinship with us.

We could each tell how comfortable we were together, and we connected.

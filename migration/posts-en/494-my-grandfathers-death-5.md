## 777

For this travel blog, I aim for about 800 characters of Japanese per post.

Curious how long the last one had run, I checked. It came out to exactly 777 characters in Japanese.

It was as if the spirit of my grandfather, who loved to gamble, were watching over me.

My grandfather and I seem to share some strong spiritual connection, but those stories belong to events a few years after this, and I'll tell them when we get there.

## Settling Up

My grandfather balanced his books beautifully.

The insurance money paid out on his death covered his debts and the funeral, and it all came out exactly even.

He'd been a sloppy, careless man all his life, but at the moment of death he squared every account and left cleanly for the next world.

By a gambler's standards, that has to be pretty cool.

## The Next Move

With the funeral more or less behind us, I decided to move on to my next step.

I'd come back to Japan this time because my grandfather's condition had gotten worse, but from that point on, my goal had been to travel around Japan.

After traveling through Thailand, India, England, and Morocco, I'd come to know a little of the world.

Of Japan, though, I knew nothing outside Osaka, where I was born and raised.

I hadn't had nearly enough of traveling the world, but heading back out into it without knowing Japan first seemed like a sorry showing for a traveler representing Japan.

But Japan is expensive.

Travel the way I had in India, and the money would be gone in no time.

Japan had to have its own way of traveling, I figured, and the answer I arrived at was a bicycle trip.

That idea also lined up with an answer I'd reached once before, when I asked myself what the greatest possible journey would be: the ultimate, I'd decided, was traveling through Africa by bicycle.

If I was going to ride through Africa someday, I figured I'd better start building experience now by riding around Japan.

## The Cave Guru

H had apparently trained under this cave guru for about four months.

And this was no half-hearted yoga class, either — from what he said, you lived in the cave and trained from morning till night without a break.

He'd learned breathing techniques there too, and he showed me what they could do.

After a few minutes of hard, forceful breathing, he pushed out every last bit of air, until his stomach practically touched his back.

Then he held it like that for several minutes.

Sucking in his stomach that far was impressive enough, but holding his breath for minutes with nothing left in his lungs was unreal.

There was no getting around it: the training had been the real thing.

## Sex Training

As part of the practice, apparently, the practitioners had sex with one another.

But it wasn't about fun or anything erotic. By his account, they would train, then have sex for hours in particular positions to build up spiritual energy.

Ejaculating during the act was absolutely forbidden. If they felt close, they would do a headstand and steady their breathing until the sensation pulled back.

## A Question

In the middle of telling me all this, H turned to me and asked a question.

"Do you want sex?"

Thrown by the question, I gave a half-baked answer: "Uh, well, yeah, I guess."

His reply surprised me even more.

"Me, I'm done with it. Got bored. I've already had a lifetime's worth of sex."

That answer honestly stunned me.

At the time I was twenty-two, and H was twenty-five.

Here was a twenty-five-year-old man at the very height of his vigor, and at that age he had already risen above sexual desire.

At twenty-two, I was naturally interested in sex, but the way H talked, as someone beyond worldly desires, drew me to him in an instant.

This bike trip in particular was partly a trip to get over a breakup, so the gap between him and me — still moping, bitter at the girlfriend who'd dumped me — was painful.

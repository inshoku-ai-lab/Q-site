## After DMT

After my session, I-chan went next.

She had gone somewhere deep too, apparently, but it was not something she could put into words, and all that came out was a sigh and the word incredible.

I talked DMT over with a few other friends, but it was like two people describing last night's dreams to each other, and nothing ever came through.

I never managed a conversation about it with anyone else that satisfied me, but inside myself it meant a great deal.

The experience began to free me from the framework of time.

Until then, time had simply been there as the frame of the world, absolute and inviolable; after this, I understood that it stretches or shrinks according to your own perception.

And in a world like the "next world," time does not exist, and the infinite and the instant are both there at once — a thing out of a Zen koan, which I understood as a sensation rather than as a thought.

In my head I understood none of it, but the experience itself was absolute.

That experience was what began to loosen the closed, fixed ideas I held about the material world.

## Curiosity

DMT set my curiosity on fire.

But S said it was better to wait a month or so after doing it once, so when the month was up I asked him for another session.

Even without the waiting, the stuff was expensive enough that it was not something you could do often.

I-chan was not that interested, so I was the only one going in for a second session.

Having been through it once, I had no fear of the unknown this time, and it all went easily.

## The Second Session

With S guiding me, I went into the other dimension.

Just as before, an infinite geometric mandala spread out, and a strange melody filled the place.

And just as before, I was not looking at that world from outside it — I had become the world itself.

What was different this time was that the presence in that other dimension had a will of its own.

That will was there, in the middle of the geometric patterns spreading inside my eyes.

And it was sending out toward me a wave of love, brimming with warm acceptance.

At the center of the other dimension was a being of love.

Its light was a warm color, something like pink, and it radiated a warm, gentle energy.

My second session closed in a warm feeling, wrapped in kindness.

## The Risks of Goa

The dangers lurking in Goa were not only traffic accidents; having thieves break into your house was another crisis that could happen to you.

R and T, a couple, had come to Goa and made their home in the forest near the town of Mapusa, far from the travelers' hub.

What they wanted was not the crowded beach life but a quiet life in the forest, built around what they ate, and for that this was exactly the right place.

A stand-alone house of a reasonable size, with a kitchen and a garden.

They could live comfortably there, and this far out from the travelers' hub the risk of a thief getting in would be low — that was how they figured it.

You had to think about the risk of thieves in advance when you chose a house. That was how practical a problem they were in Goa.

But even that house, far out from the hub, got broken into.

## The Thief

It was late one night.

They had spent their days as they always did and were asleep in bed when the thief came to their house.

He quietly lifted the tiles off the roof and worked his way down the wall into the room.

T never noticed the sound and slept on, but R picked up on it and woke.

The thief moved so quietly it made you picture a professional.

The call R made right then was to go on pretending to be asleep.

In the end the thief could not find anything worth money, and got away with nothing but a speaker and a portable music player.

There is no telling how right that call was, but their lives were never put in danger.

## Drugs

Another risk of the Goa life was drugs.

There was trouble with the police and trouble between dealers, but trouble from overdoses and from people getting disoriented came up constantly as well.

Among my Japanese friends there was a group known as the XX Crew — XX being an upscale residential district in Tokyo — and every one of them was the son of a very rich family, and they played hard and loud.

The son of the director-general of some ministry or other, the son of a world-famous camera company, the family behind a famous clothing brand, people in the television business — brought up in freedom and elegance, when it came to playing they took it all the way, not knowing how to stop.
